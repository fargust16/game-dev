## 1. The first-day trial

The trial is the slice's first Allegiance fork (11 *The fork*): an investigation of Thomas Wren's disappearance, whose end is a choice between the faction and the path taken alone. It has no timer: it waits until the invasion (owner, S5-5).

### 1.1 Doors in

Any one opens the trial; each gives the character the same first note, *Thomas Wren is missing*.

| Door | Where and when | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| The Notice Board | "Missing — Thomas Wren, clerk", posted by his sister, day 1 from 08:00 | The note and Lottie's name |
| Lottie Wren | Her tenement on Draught Lane by day, the Soot & Bell 18:00–22:00 | The note, Thomas's room, and his key (§1.2) |
| Mr Ashgrave | The faction's house on Quiet Whistle Garden, 07:00–23:00 | The trial itself: "Find out what took Thomas Wren, and bring me what he left behind" |

Ashgrave offers the trial to any character who is Factionless and not initiated. He says the faction "keeps the church's quiet business", and nothing of practitioners until the character has joined. A character who first meets Lottie can still go to Ashgrave; one who never meets Ashgrave can still find the cache, and then has only the path alone.

### 1.2 Thomas's room

Thomas lodged alone on the second floor of a tenement on Draught Lane, in Lower Veil. His door opens into the Dwelling scene, furnished as a poor single room.

- **Lottie's key.** Lottie gives it when the character says it will look for her brother, with no check. With the key the door is no break-in.
- **Without the key** the door is a lock, a Reaction Check of Theft and Dexterity with Lockpicks at stage 9, and a break-in by every rule of [10.3](../../../10.3%20Stealth%20and%20Detection.md) §6.1–6.2: a neighbour outside who sees it fetches a Warden.
- **What lies in the open** (each a Physical Trace, entered in the Character Notes when looked at):
  - the mill's pay ledger, one page cut out, the last line "W. — advance refused";
  - an unsent letter to Lottie: "Don't look for me, Lot. There's a door opening, and I mean to be on the right side of it when it does.";
  - a cold grate with a burnt corner of a page, the word "General" still legible.
- **The cache** lies under a loose floorboard beneath the bed. It is authored, not a Hidden Cache (§5). Within 2 m of the bed the character notices it by a Perception check at stage 9, rolled once each time it comes within 2 m. Lottie's line, when asked about her brother's habits, is an Argument that finds it without a roll: "He hid his wages from Father under the boards, when we were small."
- **One trace slot.** The room is one of the sources of a Ritual Weak Point's trace (11 *Finding the traces*): which trace, if any, lies here is C6's, by the seed. Until S6 the slot is empty, and the trial still passes the trace "with no sign of what it is for" (11) once C6 fills it.

### 1.3 What the cache holds

|---|---|---|
| *Formula of Limit 9, Император* | 0 | A note: the recipe of §2; an entry in the Character Notes |

### 1.4 The fork

- **Hand it in.** Ashgrave takes the two formulas and the letter, and the character keeps the coins ("The church has no use for a clerk's savings"). He sends the character to the old chapel: Father Ambrose Hale must vouch for it (11, S5-5). The priest asks one question with no check ("Why do you want to serve?"), any answer will do, and gives his word, a note in the Character Notes. Back at the faction's house, Ashgrave admits the character: **Faction Entry** into the faction, the church's hidden police of practitioners.
  - The *Formula of Limit 9, Жрица*, a note (§2).
  - The **Faction Gift**: three items set by the World Seed, firearms and armour only while melee is withheld, of which the character takes one (19 §6.2).
  - A recruit's purse of 1 000 bronze.
  - The faction's bench, free (§4), and the faction's tasks (§3.3).
  - Once the character is at Limit 9, Ashgrave gives the *Formula of Limit 8, Жрица* when spoken to.
- **Keep it.** The character keeps both Император formulas: the path taken alone. It cannot join by saying it found nothing: a lie needs an Argument, and there is none (owner, S5-5). Ashgrave, told nothing, says "A clerk doesn't vanish and leave nothing. Look again."
- **The fork closes** when the character drinks a Limit 9 potion of either Arcana, since a character takes one Arcana (11). Until then a keeper may still hand the cache in.
- **Ashgrave without the cache.** A character who brings only the letter to Lottie or the open traces hears "Look again"; the trial ends only with the cache.

### 1.6 As built in S5c.4

Built on 2026-11-13. The agent's readings are in [99](../../../99%20Open%20Questions.md) VS-TRIAL.

- **The state.** The trial is told in the world's state. Lottie Wren's, Mr Ashgrave's and Father Hale's conversations (`LottieDialogue`, `AshgraveDialogue`, `HaleDialogue`) raise its keys, and `SliceTrial` answers each key the moment it is raised, with what that step brings. A load restores the keys without raising them, so nothing is given twice.
- **The first note.** The Notice Board's own Wren notice (`board.wren`) is the first note from every door in. Ashgrave's offer and Lottie's key tell the same note.
- **Lottie.** She is talked to at both her posts, beside her tenement's street door by day and at the Soot & Bell in the evening. "I'll look for him" gives Thomas's key. Asking about his habits gives the Argument about the boards.
- **Thomas's door.** The door from Draught Lane (`missing-room.street`) says *Locked* until it is opened once. With the key, E opens it for good and goes through. Picking the lock comes with S5c.5.
- **Thomas's room.** The room is the authored MissingRoom scene, not the generated Dwelling. Three Physical Traces of no POI lie in it: the ledger and the letter on the table, and the burnt page in the cold grate. The loose board lies under the bed. The box that stood for the cache is a plain box.
- **The loose board.** Each time the character comes within 2 m, one Perception check at stage 9 is rolled, at difficulty 2, with Intelligence as its secondary attribute, and the check trains them. Lottie's Argument finds the board with no roll. *E  Lift the loose board* then takes the cache.
- **Ashgrave.** He offers the trial to a Factionless, uninitiated character. "About Thomas Wren…" gets "Look again" until the cache is handed in. Handing in the found cache takes the two formulas and the letter. "Father Hale has vouched for me" is Faction Entry. Once the character has drunk the Limit 9 potion of Жрица, he gives the formula of Limit 8. Once the character has an Arcana, the offer, the hand-in and the entry are gone.
- **Father Hale.** "Mr Ashgrave sent me" opens his question, and any of the three answers vouches. His word is a note.
- **The Faction Gift.** Faction Entry gives the formula of Limit 9, Жрица, and the recruit's purse. When the conversation ends, a page offers three different bases at stage 9: the pistol, the rifle, or one of the 24 armour pieces. They are set by the World Seed's own stream: Magic 70 % (one or two modifiers), Rare 28 % (three), Epic 2 % (four). A firearm comes with a plain Cartridge Pouch. One is taken. A page closed unchosen opens again at the end of the next conversation with Ashgrave.
- **Not yet built.** The faction's tasks and the Masters' orders (§3) come with S5c.7. The trace slot of §1.2 waits for C6 (S6).

## 5. Hidden Caches

Owner, 2026-11-13: caches lie hidden across the city and outside its walls, found by Perception, and training Perception and a little Luck (07.1 §3). Authoring defaults:

- **Placement.** About 40 candidate spots (§5.1). The seed chooses 20 for a run: at least 6 outside the walls, and at most 3 in one district. A third of the chosen, by the seed, are locked.
- **Noticing.** A cache shows nothing until noticed. When the character is within 3 m and sees the spot, a hidden Perception check at the spot's stage is rolled; on success a faint glint shows and a Screen Message says "Something's hidden here" (07.1 §11). A failed roll is rolled again only after the character has gone 10 m away and come back.
- **Opening.** An unlocked cache opens with a press. A locked one is a Reaction Check of Theft and Dexterity with Lockpicks at the spot's stage; a failure breaks a pick, as any lock.
- **Growth.** Noticing a cache gives Perception the XP of a check at its stage, and Luck 2 XP (07.1 §3). Opening a locked one trains Theft as any lock.
- **Contents**, rolled by the seed when the run begins, so a save keeps them:

| Share | Unlocked cache | Locked cache |
|---|---|---|
| 60 % | 50–300 bronze | 150–600 bronze |
| 25 % | A bandage, an antidote, a splint, lockpicks, or 3 herbs | Two of them |
| 10 % | A Magic ring, necklace or belt by the item catalogue | The same, or a Rare one 1 time in 4 |
| 5 % | A note, a Tip about the world (a rumour, never about a Weak Point) | The same and 100 bronze |

Luck multiplies the coins by (1 + Luck / 100), as in 19's recovery. At these shares 20 caches hold about 5 000–6 000 bronze of worth in a run: about two Notice Board days, a help but never the price of a Limit 8 ingredient.
