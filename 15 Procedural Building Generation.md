# Purpose

Long-horizon architecture for **procedural, modular buildings** after the Architecture Pivot, generated by **Piece-Based Assembly**: small hand-authored modules snapped together small → large, the same philosophy [16](16%20Architecture%20Pivot.md)/[17](17%20Game%20Systems%20Architecture.md) already lock for the overworld (Valheim-shaped: seed-placed pieces, not one hand-tuned continuous mesh).

Scope:

- **Exterior shells / landmarks** on the seeded overworld;
- **Interior scenes** (separate, free third-person, no grid);
- optional later DNA / room recipes for interior scene content.

This document is the authority for *how building content is generated as systems*.  
[16 Architecture Pivot](16%20Architecture%20Pivot.md) is the authority for *scene graph / movement / combat presentation*.  
[14 World Generation Roadmap](14%20World%20Generation%20Roadmap.md) is the authority for *implementation order* (**next code default: Overworld V0**).  
[13 City Generation](13%20City%20Generation.md) (deprecated) is **not** a reuse source for this pipeline.

Success question:

> Can we generate distinct exterior POIs and interior scenes by snapping small authored Pieces together from Building DNA — without authoring finished whole-building prefabs, without an algorithmic subdivision solver, and without baking continuous in-world GridMap interiors?

# Design Philosophy

## Goal

Not beautiful facades, and not a pure math simulation of a city either.  
**Explorable buildings as two products:** overworld exterior presence + separate interior scenes with secrets, NPC anchors, and believable Victorian / industrial-occult mood — assembled from a library of small, human-authored, real-reference pieces, the way a person actually reads believability: recognisable rooms and street modules repeated with variation, not a procedurally solved floor plan.

## Piece-Based Assembly (core mechanism)

The generation mechanism, at every scale, is the same small → large composition rule:

```text
Piece  (a small hand-authored module: a room segment, a floor slice, a street/block module)
  → carries Snap Sockets on its boundary (typed connection points: door-width, wall-run, street-frontage, corridor-end, …)
  → an assembler seeded from World Seed + Building DNA picks a legal next Piece for each open socket
  → Pieces compose into a Room (interior) or a Plot (exterior/settlement)
  → Rooms/Plots compose into a Floor / Building
  → Buildings compose into a Block / Settlement
  → Settlements compose into an overworld POI / zone
```

Reference model: **Valheim's own room/dungeon piece system** — a curated kit of small hand-built pieces with sockets, assembled by a lightweight seeded algorithm — not a from-scratch procedural solver. This is deliberately simpler than algorithmic floor-plan subdivision: authors control what a Piece looks like and where it may connect; the algorithm only decides *which* legal piece goes where. This is also why it reads as believable — every piece is real-reference-authored, not derived from an abstract split.

## Authority chain (locked — post-pivot)

```text
Building DNA
    ↓
District / Biome Rules + Plot or POI slot
    ↓
Exterior Footprint / Shell (overworld)
    ↓
Door / portal link → Interior Scene id
    ↓
Interior Floor Stack + Floor Plans (scene-local, built by Piece-Based Assembly)
    ↓
Room Graph (interior scene data — the *result* of piece assembly, not a subdivision algorithm)
    ↓
Vertical Connections
    ↓
Openings (doors / windows)
    ↓
Facade Modules + Roof Modules (overworld shell)
    ↓
Materials / Age / Damage
    ↓
Interior Dress (furniture / props / lighting)
    ↓
Secrets Overlay
    ↓
Gameplay Bindings (sockets, loot, quests, NPC roles)
```

Confront combat does **not** use interior geometry as grid truth — arenas use Theme Packs ([16](16%20Architecture%20Pivot.md)).

## Hard rules

1. **Never ship whole buildings as prefabs.** Prefabs are only *Pieces* (a room segment, a door bay, a stair flight, a street module) — never a finished building or a finished room.
2. **Overworld / interiors:** free third-person collision spaces — not GridMap walkability as world law.
3. **Interiors are separate scenes** — not continuous in-world enterable fabric.
4. **Deterministic** from world seed + building id / plot id forks.
5. **Reuse Pieces, not buildings or whole rooms.** Archetypes are recipes over a shared Piece Kit; a Room is an assembly *result*, not an authored unit.
6. **Snap Sockets are the only legal connection.** An assembler may never join two pieces that do not share a compatible socket type — this is what keeps output structurally sound without a subdivision solver.
7. **Secrets are DNA overlays**, not a late optional art pass.
8. **Scale via the Piece Kit first, archetypes second.** Grow the kit and the room library before writing more archetype documents.
9. Pre-pivot OrganicTown continuous GridMap bake path is **deprecated** — not a reuse source, not a bridge, not a basis for this pipeline ([13](13%20City%20Generation.md)).

## Nuance on "no prefabs"

Forbidden: drop-in Victorian house / tavern / church kits as generation authority.  
Allowed and required: a modular Piece Kit (`Assets/Art/TownDress/Modules/…`) snapped to generated openings and shells — see `PROCEDURAL_TOWN_ASSET_LIBRARY.md`.

# Critique of Category-First Roadmaps

A roadmap that does Residential → Commercial → … → Interiors → Secrets is inverted.

Problems:

- Interiors and Room Graph are **core systems**, not a late phase.
- Secrets/story are **cross-cutting DNA layers**, not after every civic type exists.
- Faction / Special buildings are usually **overlays** (cult basement under warehouse), not unique engines.
- Writing a full spec for every archetype before Vertical Slice wastes design budget — and produces exactly the over-designed, under-built pattern this project is moving away from.

Improved order: **Piece Kit + systems → Vertical Slice archetypes → expand the kit on demand**.

# Architecture Roadmap (improved)

Implementation respects [16](16%20Architecture%20Pivot.md) + [14](14%20World%20Generation%20Roadmap.md): **Overworld V0 → Interior V0** before deep building DNA. Systems below can be designed now; code lands in micro-plans after those rails exist.

## Phase 0 — Contracts & Truth (post-pivot)

**Deliverables**

- Exterior shell / POI slot on overworld
- Door link → Interior Scene id
- Interior scene free-move contract (no grid)
- Theme Pack ids for arenas spawned from building contexts (combat only)

**Acceptance**

- Player can approach an exterior, enter an interior scene, exit back
- Confront from interior/overworld uses arena Theme Pack — not interior grid bake

**Pre-pivot note:** Continuous OrganicTown enterable + HideWalls + GridMap path is deprecated archive only.

## Phase 1 — Core Architecture Systems

Reusable generators before new building categories.

| System | Responsibility |
|--------|----------------|
| **Building DNA** | Seeded identity that drives all later stages |
| **District Rules** | Wealth, style, density, crime, occult pressure |
| **Plot → Footprint** | Yard / court / rear alley tags |
| **Floor Stack** | Storey count, basement/attic flags, floor-to-floor rules |
| **Piece Kit (Room Library)** | Parametric small pieces with Snap Sockets (size ranges, adjacency, socket types) |
| **Room Graph Generator** | Piece selection + Snap Socket connection from DNA + footprint (see Core Systems below) |
| **Corridor Generator** | Circulation pieces inserted where the graph needs them |
| **Opening Generator** | Doors, windows, service hatches |
| **Stair / Vertical Generator** | Stair/ladder/trapdoor Pieces |
| **Shell / Facade Module Binder** | Snap modules to opening sockets |
| **Roof Generator** | Hip/gable/flat + chimneys by footprint + wealth |
| **Material / Age / Damage** | Palette from DNA |
| **Interior Dress** | Furniture/prop/light recipes per room type |
| **Secrets Overlay** | Hidden rooms, false walls, tunnels (DNA-gated) |
| **Gameplay Binder** | NPC/loot/quest markers onto sockets |
| **Interior Scene Compiler** | Piece graph → interior scene layout / greybox (free-move) |
| **Exterior Shell Binder** | Footprint → overworld shell + door portal |

**Exit criteria:** one DNA → one Worker House and one Tavern, deterministic exterior + interior scene, assembled from Pieces, socketted, roofed greybox shell, no whole-building or whole-room prefab.

## Phase 2 — Vertical Slice Archetypes

Prove the full stack on few buildings that teach the world.

| Archetype | Why first |
|-----------|-----------|
| Worker House | Dense residential fabric |
| Alley Shop / Booth | Tiny commercial, street edge |
| Tavern / Inn | Social hub + possible smuggler DNA |
| Apothecary | Curiosity + alchemy secret overlay |
| Small Church / Chapel | Public ritual + crypt potential |

These are authored fresh as Piece Kits for this pipeline — there is no upgrade path from the retired D1 templates ([13](13%20City%20Generation.md)); building the first Piece Kit for these five is new work.

## Later Archetype Expansion (on demand, not now)

Beyond the five V1 archetypes above, more building categories (residential variety, commercial fabric, industrial/infrastructure, civic/public, authority/force, sacred/death, government/elite, faction/occult overlays, special landmarks) are a **backlog, not a roadmap** — do not draft full specs for them now. Expand the Piece Kit and add an Archetype Card (see below) only when a specific Vertical Slice or content need justifies it. Faction/occult content in particular should usually be injected as DNA `Secrets` / `FactionOwnership` onto an ordinary archetype rather than becoming its own standalone building type.

City-scale concerns (bake caching, LOD, lazy-on-enter interior generation, prop memory budgets) are deferred until multiple settlements actually exist; see Performance Principles below for the standing rules that already apply at small scale.

# Building DNA

Every instance carries a seeded DNA record. DNA is the single upstream driver.

## Fields (V1 set)

| Field | Type | Influences |
|-------|------|------------|
| BuildingType / Archetype | enum | Piece Kit selection |
| DistrictId | id | Style, materials, street manners |
| Wealth | Poor → Noble | Size, finish, prop density |
| Purpose | enum | Public hours, socket roles |
| FactionOwnership | none / soft / secret | Guard sockets, locked doors, overlays |
| ConstructionEra | decade band | Facade language, materials |
| ArchitecturalStyle | Victorian / Industrial / Gothic / Hybrid | Piece set filter |
| OccupantProfile | count + roles | NPC sockets |
| CrimeLevel | 0–3 | Bars, rear exits, loot risk |
| OccultLevel | 0–3 | Hidden rooms, ritual props, wrong geometry hints |
| StoryImportance | 0–3 | Whether C1 may bind a Decision Node |
| DamageLevel | 0–3 | Broken modules, boarded windows |
| SecretsMask | flags | Tunnel, false wall, attic cache, basement lab… |
| Seed | ulong | Fork root for all child RNGs |

## Propagation rule

No stage may invent identity that contradicts DNA.  
If a tavern has `SecretsMask.SmugglerTunnel`, the Piece assembler must reserve a socket for it; Facade must not seal the only escape without a readable alternate.

# Core Systems (detail)

## Piece Kit (Room Library)

Pieces are small, parametric, hand-authored modules — not unique meshes per building.

### Shared piece kinds (starter set)

Living: Bedroom, Kitchen, Dining, Parlour, Hall, Stairwell, Landing, Attic Room, Basement Store  
Work: Shop Floor, Counter Room, Workshop, Office, Study, Library Stack, Classroom, Dormitory  
Care: Ward, Surgery, Mortuary Prep  
Force: Cell, Guard Room, Armoury  
Industry: Machine Floor, Loading Bay, Boiler Room, Chemical Store  
Hospitality: Bar, Taproom, Guest Room, Kitchen (service)  
Occult: Alchemy Lab, Ritual Room, Reliquary, Crypt, Hidden Vault, Séance Parlour, Dream Den  
Circulation: Corridor, Courtyard, Service Alley Access

Each Piece defines:

- Footprint size (fixed or small range)
- Snap Sockets on its boundary (type + count: street door, corridor end, wall-run, stair core, …)
- Opening rules (street door? barred window?)
- Socket role hints (NPC/loot/quest)
- Furniture / light recipe
- Wealth/occult variants (same piece, different dress)
- Whether it may host a Secret child piece

## Room Graph Generator (Piece-Based Assembly)

1. Select the archetype's Piece Kit from DNA (style/wealth/era tags filter which Pieces are eligible).
2. Place a required entry Piece that satisfies Purpose (street-facing room when Purpose requires public access).
3. Walk outward from open Snap Sockets: for each open socket, deterministically pick (seed fork `"pieces"`) a compatible Piece from the kit; adjacency/size rules gate which picks are legal.
4. Stop when the DNA room-count / floor-area budget is met, or no legal Piece remains for an open socket — cap it with a plain wall piece.
5. Insert Corridor pieces wherever circulation degree/distance rules fail.
6. Place vertical-core Pieces (stairs/ladders) before facade lock.
7. Compile the resulting piece graph to per-floor cell paint (`BuildingTemplate`-compatible data shape, not the retired BuildingTemplate content).

Failure = retry with a relaxed Piece filter, shrink optional pieces — never orphan the entrance.

## Vertical Connections

- Stair/ladder/trapdoor **Pieces** occupy reserved vertical cores, selected the same way as any other Piece
- Basement if DNA or archetype allows (Wealth/Occult/Industrial)
- Attic if roof type + Wealth allow
- Ladders/trapdoors for poor / secret variants

## Facade & Roof

- Openings from Room Graph street edges
- Module bind: door/window/trim Pieces from TownDress library
- Roof from footprint OBB + style; chimneys from hearth/kitchen/boiler rooms

## Secrets Overlay

Runs after Room Graph assembly, before final bake:

| Secret | Typical host | Readable residue |
|--------|--------------|------------------|
| False-wall room | Study, Vestry, Cellar | Misaligned trim, cold draft prop |
| Smuggler tunnel | Tavern cellar, Warehouse | Hatch under crates |
| Alchemy lab | Apothecary back, Mansion basement | Chemical smell props, locked door |
| Crypt extension | Church | Worn steps, memorial mismatch |
| Attic cache | Worker House, Medium House | Disturbed dust, padlock |
| Cult circle | Warehouse, Abandoned chapel | Wrong geometry, stained floor decal |

Secrets must leave **world residue** (Confront Constraints / Information Sources aligned) — not invisible designer teleports.

## Interior Scene Compiler

Compile rules (post-pivot):

- Piece graph → navigable free-move interior greybox (collision meshes, not GridMap world truth)
- Door Piece cells become portal anchors back to overworld / nested scenes
- Secret door Pieces become diegetic interacts with residue
- Arena Theme Pack id may be suggested by DNA (tavern → `Tavern` arena) but combat geometry is separate

# Archetype Specification Process

Do **not** create fifty full documents on day one.

## Process

1. Stabilize Phase 1 systems + Piece Kit.
2. For each archetype that enters production: fill an **Archetype Card** (short) then expand to full spec only when scheduled.
3. Store cards under `unity/Assets/Plans/buildings/` as `ARCHETYPE_<Name>.md` when written.

## Archetype Card (required fields)

- Purpose / gameplay role  
- Typical district  
- Footprint & floors  
- Piece Kit list (which Pieces this archetype draws from)  
- Vertical rules  
- DNA defaults & rare variants  
- Socket roles  
- Secret overlays allowed  
- Dependencies on reusable systems  
- V1 / V2 / V3 depth  

## Full Spec (later, per scheduled archetype)

Use the full checklist when a card graduates:

Purpose, gameplay role, district, footprint, floors, exterior, interior room graph, vertical circulation, room types, furniture/prop/lighting, window/door/roof rules, basement/attic, hidden rooms, passages, rituals, faction ownership, NPC archetypes, loot, quest hooks, rare/district/wealth/destroyed variants, performance, generation algorithm, system dependencies.

### V1 production set (write full specs first)

1. Worker House  
2. Tavern  
3. Apothecary  
4. Alley Shop  
5. Small Church

# Performance Principles

- Generate **data** for the town; **mesh** near player / playable radius first  
- Cap prop instances by Wealth and room importance  
- Share materials; vary via tint / decal / damage  
- No per-building unique lightmap dependency for V1  
- Secrets that are never near player stay as data flags until activation
