# Purpose

Long-horizon architecture for **procedural, modular buildings** after the Architecture Pivot, generated by **Piece-Based Assembly**: small hand-authored modules snapped together small → large ([16](16%20Architecture%20Pivot.md)/[17](17%20Game%20Systems%20Architecture.md) Valheim-shaped composition).

This document owns **two related products** with different schedules:

| Product | Role | When |
|---------|------|------|
| **Exterior Shell Grammar** | Overworld city fabric: silhouette, street manners, district character. Most buildings are **background shells** — not enterable from the overworld. | Shell Sandbox now (parallel lab); Settlement hook later |
| **Interior Piece Assembly** | Separate interior scenes for **gameplay-gated** buildings only (`InteriorPolicy.Portal`). Room Graph, secrets, dress. | After Interior V0 rails |

[16 Architecture Pivot](16%20Architecture%20Pivot.md) owns scene graph / movement / combat presentation.  
[14 World Generation Roadmap](14%20World%20Generation%20Roadmap.md) owns world-gen code order (**next default: Overworld V0**). Shell sandbox does **not** replace Overworld V0.  
[13 City Generation](13%20City%20Generation.md) (deprecated) is **not** a reuse source.

Success question:

> From a small curated Exterior Piece Kit + Building DNA + Plot/StreetContext, can we assemble many believable, district-coherent overworld shells — and, only when needed, open a portal to a separate interior scene assembled the same way — without whole-building prefabs, without algorithmic floor-plan solvers, and without continuous in-world enterable fabric?

# Design Philosophy

## Goal

Not realism-theatre construction, and not a pure math city.

**Most buildings exist to form streets, districts, landmarks, and exploration rhythm.**  
**Few buildings** open into interior scenes with secrets and NPC anchors.

Victorian / industrial-occult mood ([11](11%20Vertical%20Slice.md)) is the first Architectural Style.

## Hybrid approach (locked)

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| Art | Hand-authored **Pieces** on a fixed modular grid |
| Parametric slots | Plot bays, floor count, wealth — constrain, do not invent mesh |
| Grammar | Ordered assembly (foundation → storeys → roof → vertical → trim) |
| Archetypes | Small cards over shared kits |
| Landmarks | Handcrafted exceptions (`LandmarkClass.Landmark`) |

Rejected as primary paths: pure parametric mesh generation; handcrafted hundreds of whole buildings; OrganicTown continuous bake; WFC-as-MVP.

## Piece-Based Assembly (core mechanism)

```text
Piece  (hand-authored module + Snap Sockets)
  → assembler (World Seed + Building DNA) picks legal next Piece
  → Exterior: Pieces → storey rings → ShellInstance on a Plot
  → Interior (Portal only): Pieces → Room → Floor → Interior Scene
  → Buildings → Block / Settlement → overworld POI
```

Reference: Valheim room/dungeon pieces — curated kit + lightweight seeded selection, not subdivision solvers.

## Two authority chains

### Exterior Shell Grammar (primary overworld product)

```text
Building DNA (+ DistrictProfile bias)
    ↓
PlotFootprint + StreetContext
    ↓
Archetype select (legal for plot / district / street role)
    ↓
Exterior Shell Grammar
    foundation → ground storey → upper storeys → roof → vertical → trim
    ↓
ShellInstance (+ optional PortalSocket)
```

Background fabric stops here. No Room Graph. No interior geometry in overworld.

### Interior Piece Assembly (gameplay buildings only)

```text
PortalSocket (from shell with InteriorPolicy.Portal)
    ↓
Interior Floor Stack + Piece Kit (rooms)
    ↓
Room Graph (assembly result)
    ↓
Vertical / openings / dress / secrets / gameplay bindings
```

Confront runs in place on a Combat Grid built from the scene ([16](16%20Architecture%20Pivot.md)), so generated pieces must carry what the grid needs. Fences, boundaries, lamps, and props get colliders, which they lack today.

**The slice's Dwellings** (owner, 2026-11-06). In the Vertical Slice every dwelling is a Portal building, because breaking in is gameplay ([10.3 Stealth and Detection](10.3%20Stealth%20and%20Detection.md) §6.1). One shared **Dwelling** scene runs this assembly on entry from the house's archetype and seed: one home of two to four rooms on the floor of its door, a single flat in a tenement, built from Victorian Interiors' pieces on its 3 m module (owner, 2026-11-07; BK_AlchemistHouse before), a rich house on as many floors, two or three, as it shows outside ([VICTORIAN_INTERIORS_PLAN.md](unity/Assets/Plans/VICTORIAN_INTERIORS_PLAN.md)). The same house always gives the same rooms, and its state is kept for the run. The seven buildings that open into their own interiors stay hand-assembled from the same kit. Rooms by archetype, authoring defaults ([VEILMOUTH_CITY_PLAN.md](unity/Assets/Plans/VEILMOUTH_CITY_PLAN.md) §8): a terrace has a hall, parlour, kitchen and bedroom; a tenement flat a room and a kitchen, or one room; a shop a shop room, back room and kitchen; a villa a hall, parlour, dining room, kitchen and study; a warehouse a store and an office. Built 2026-11-06 (city plan §16): `DwellingPlanner` writes the plan from the house's key and the seed stream `slice.dwellings`, and `DwellingAssembler` builds it; Veilmouth has 265 dwelling doors. Keeping a house's state for the run waits for breaking in.

## Hard rules

1. **Never ship whole buildings as generation authority.** Prefabs are Pieces only. Landmarks are an explicit handcrafted exception class, not the fabric path.
2. **Overworld shells are exteriors only** — no continuous enterable interiors.
3. **Interiors are separate scenes** when `InteriorPolicy.Portal`.
4. **Deterministic** from world seed + plot/building id forks.
5. **Reuse Pieces, not finished buildings.**
6. **Snap Sockets are the only legal connection.**
7. **Secrets are DNA overlays** on interior assembly — not a late art pass.
8. **Scale kit first, archetypes second.**
9. OrganicTown continuous GridMap path is **deprecated** ([13](13%20City%20Generation.md)).
10. **Background fabric does not require a Room Graph.** Only Portal buildings pay interior cost.

## Nuance on "no prefabs"

Forbidden: drop-in Victorian house kits as fabric authority.  
Allowed: modular Piece Kit under `Assets/Art/TownDress/Modules/…` — see `PROCEDURAL_TOWN_ASSET_LIBRARY.md`.  
Allowed: one-off Landmark prefabs tagged `LandmarkClass.Landmark`.

# Modular Grid (MVP lock)

| Unit | Size | Use |
|------|------|-----|
| **Bay** | 2 m | Plot width/depth; wall/window/door module width |
| **Storey** | 3 m | Floor-to-floor height |

All exterior structural pieces snap to this grid. Compatibility = socket type + bay size + style tag + wealth band.

# Exterior Shell Grammar

## Piece categories (exterior kit)

| Category | Examples | MVP |
|----------|----------|-----|
| Structural | Wall straight, corner, end, party wall, foundation | Yes |
| Facade fill | Window bay, door bay, blank bay, shopfront bay | Yes |
| Roof | Gable, flat (+ hip later) | Gable + flat |
| Vertical | Chimney | Yes |
| Decorations / trim | Cornice, sill, sign stub | Wealth-gated |
| Balcony / dormer / tower | — | Post-MVP |

**Snap Socket types (exterior):** `WallRun`, `Corner`, `StreetFront`, `DoorBay`, `WindowBay`, `RoofEdge`, `ChimneyPad`, `Portal`.

## StreetContext

Each plot face: `Street | Alley | Yard | Square | Party`.

Rules:

- Door / shopfront on `Street` (and `Square`) fronts.
- `Party` faces: blank / cheap walls.
- Corner plots: two street faces; grammar places entrance on primary front.
- Through-lots (street front + back): primary entrance on higher-priority face (Street > Square > Alley).

## PlotFootprint (V1 shapes)

| Shape | MVP |
|-------|-----|
| Rectangle | Yes |
| Corner (rect + two street faces) | Yes (sandbox iteration) |
| L / U / courtyard | Deferred |

Fields: `widthBays`, `depthBays`, `shape`, `orientation`, street mask / StreetContext.

## LandmarkClass

| Class | Generator |
|-------|-----------|
| `Background` | Full Exterior Shell Grammar |
| `Notable` | Grammar + higher variation / trim |
| `Landmark` | Handcrafted prefab + DNA stub; skips fabric grammar |

## Assembly algorithm

```text
1. Plot + StreetContext given (sandbox authored; later Settlement)
2. Fork seed: district → plot → building → modules
3. Select Archetype legal for plot + district + street role
4. Sample DNA (floors, wealth, style) in archetype ranges; clamp by DistrictProfile height/wealth bias
5. Grammar (deterministic):
   a. Foundation
   b. Each bay: Wall piece first, then optional Opening overlay (Window/Door/Shopfront)
      — openings never replace the wall; street rhythm is regular (no random Blank gaps)
   c. Ground storey doors / shopfronts from StreetContext
   d. Roof from style + footprint
   e. Vertical (chimney if residential / wealth allows)
   f. Trim / string course from wealth + district
6. Emit ShellInstance; PortalSocket only if InteriorPolicy != None
```

Deferred facade dress (dormers, stoop stairs, fences/gates, real window variants): see `BUILDING_SHELL_SANDBOX_V0.md`.

## Controlled variation

Randomize (seeded): facade bay pattern, window/door variant, floors in range, roof in style set, trim density, chimney 0–2.  
Lock / correlate: bay size, storey height, style palette, street door side, district height band, LandmarkClass.

# Building DNA

Upstream identity for shell and (if Portal) interior.

## Fields (shell-lean V1 + interior stubs)

| Field | Influences |
|-------|------------|
| Archetype | Piece kit / grammar recipe |
| DistrictId | Style, materials, street manners |
| Wealth (1–5) | Size bias, trim, materials, density feel |
| ArchitecturalStyle | Piece set filter |
| FloorCount | Storeys (also sampled into DNA) |
| LandmarkClass | Background / Notable / Landmark |
| InteriorPolicy | `None` \| `Portal` |
| StoryImportance | Later Decision Node bind |
| ConstructionEra / FactionOwnership / Crime / Occult / Damage / SecretsMask | Interior + residue (stubs on shell) |
| Seed | Fork root |

Propagation: no stage invents identity that contradicts DNA.

# District integration

`DistrictProfile` (weights, not only hard allow-lists):

- `wealthBias`, `density`, `heightBand`
- `styleWeights`, `archetypeWeights`
- optional material palette override

Archetypes carry `districtAffinity` weights. Selector = weighted pick among plot-legal candidates. Soft reject preferred; hard ban for wrong Landmark / military in incompatible districts.

Sandbox district ids: `Poor`, `Worker`, `Affluent`, `Rich`, `Elite`, `Commercial`.  
Each `DistrictProfileDef` drives plot size, gap/density, wealth band, floors, shop chance, chimney/roof/window/trim biases, **massing weights**, front-garden dress, and placeholder palette. Archetypes carry affinity weights per district.

Massing (anti-box): `Box`, `LShape`, `Stepped`, `RearWing` as **floor-stack footprints** (poor stays Box). Build storey 0→N; roof a part when it stops; step faces use `AtticWindow`. Exactly one ground street door. Chimneys flush on the top roof.

**HARD LOCK — shell non-overlap:** tiles abut, never intersect. Party bays empty; no double wall/window at height steps. Validator: `ShellOverlapGuard`. Spec: `unity/Assets/Plans/SHELL_NON_OVERLAP_RULE.md`.

# Interior Piece Assembly (secondary track)

Unchanged philosophy; **not** required for background fabric.

## Piece Kit (Room Library) — interior

Living / Work / Care / Force / Industry / Hospitality / Occult / Circulation pieces with Snap Sockets, furniture recipes, secret-host flags — as previously specified. Expand on demand when Portal archetypes enter production.

## Room Graph Generator

Select kit → place entry Piece → walk open sockets with seed fork `"pieces"` → corridors / vertical cores → compile to interior scene greybox (free-move). Failure = retry / shrink — never orphan entrance.

## Secrets Overlay

DNA-gated after Room Graph; must leave world residue ([08](08%20Confront%20Constraints.md) / [09](09%20Information%20Sources.md)).

## Interior Scene Compiler

Piece graph → navigable free-move scene; portals back to overworld.

# Performance Principles

- Generate **data** first; spawn mesh near player / settlement demand.
- Shared materials / atlases; PieceDef LOD slots from day one (LOD0 filled).
- Cap trim by wealth.
- No per-building unique lightmaps V1.
- Defer HLOD / mesh merge until multi-settlement pain exists.
- Background shells stay cheap: no interior compile.
