# Purpose

This document locks the **spatial foundation of the Combat Grid**:

> When Confront starts, a square grid forms around the player from the scene's real geometry. NPCs fighting elsewhere have no grid: they are simulated. Outside Confront, overworld and interior exploration are free third-person spaces without grid gameplay truth ([16 Architecture Pivot](16%20Architecture%20Pivot.md)).

# Central Rules

1. Cover and traversal come from the scene's geometry, read onto the grid when Confront starts.
2. Pathfinding and Move use grid paths (A* on walkable cells; diagonals allowed without corner-cutting).
3. Environment Actions are first-class combat actions with AP costs and failure chances.
4. Failure is valuable: a failed climb/jump can waste the Action, cause injury, or worsen position — not empty “nothing happened.”
5. V1 keeps a small Environment Action list; do not invent parkour simulator depth.
6. Outside Confront, overworld and interiors do **not** use this grid as movement truth. Diegetic interacts there are separate.

# Grid (During Confront Only)

## V1 Grid Model

- Square grid for V1 (simplest solo implementation).
- Each cell has:
  - walkability;
  - elevation / height band;
  - cover/traversal tags;
  - occupancy (creature / large prop).
- Diagonal movement costs the same as orthogonal: 1 AP a cell (authoring default, 2026-11-03). The balance simulations of [10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §5b count distance this way, so every accepted number rests on it. Position still matters, because a melee attack goes across a cell's side only and leaving an enemy's side draws its Reaction Attack.
- Vertical change uses stairs/ladders/climb tags, not free flying.

Exact cell size is technical. The [technical test](unity/Assets/Plans/COMBAT_GRID_TECH_TEST.md) supports 1 m: narrow gaps do not survive 1.5 m cells.

## Authoring Rule

When placing a crate, window, wall, coach, or stair in the world:

- it carries the tags the grid needs when a fight starts;
- it exposes allowed Environment Actions on adjacent cells;
- it does not rely on off-grid precision for gameplay truth during Confront.

Visual mesh may overhang slightly for art, but gameplay tests the grid.

## Construction Rule

When Confront starts, the grid is built from the current scene and produces:

- floor cells;
- blocked cells;
- tagged interactive cells (window, low wall, climbable, jump-gap, cover);
- concealment from vegetation.

An interior scene has its own grid. The grid reaches 30 cells from the player in every direction, 20 before the owner's direction after the play test of 2026-11-12 that everyone within 30 cells is in a fight ([10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §9). It follows the player every step. A fighter outside it drops into simulation, and one who comes inside appears on it from the next round. It is built during a real fight, at 30 cells in the slice, with 50 planned for later ([16](16%20Architecture%20Pivot.md)).

## Entering a fight

Authoring default set by the agent on 2026-11-03.

- A fight starts in place, with no cut and no fade. At the moment of hostility the world stops, the grid appears over the real scene within half a second, a short sound marks the start, and the Initiative order appears.
- Nothing shows hostility before the world does. NPCs carry no hostile mark, and no warning comes from the interface. The warning is diegetic: a guard's two warnings ([10.4](10.4%20Dialogue%20and%20Social%20Checks.md) §6), a drawn weapon, a threat spoken aloud, cultists turning toward the character.
- An ambush or a stealth attack gives no warning at all; the first blow starts the fight.
- So the player who watches the world sees a fight coming, and the one who does not learns to.

# Movement on Grid

Movement consumes AP according to the movement-cost rules in [10.2 Combat System Draft](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md), and traverses legal grid cells along a path.

Blocked by:

- walls;
- occupied cells (unless squeezing rule added later);
- missing climb/jump tags where elevation changes.

# Environment Actions

Environment Actions are contested or risky ways to use the space around a fight.

Every Environment Action has an explicit AP cost defined by its action rules. There is no separate Move / Action / Bonus category.

## V1 Environment Action Set

| Action | Typical tag | Economy | Failure examples |
|--------|-------------|---------|------------------|
| **Open / Close** | door, shutter | AP cost defined by interaction complexity | jammed, noisy |
| **Vault / Jump** | low wall, gap, crate line | AP cost | fall short, prone/Hurt |
| **Climb Up** | climbable wall, ladder, pipe | AP cost | slip, fall Hurt |
| **Climb Through Window** | window entry | AP cost | stuck, cut Hurt |
| **Mantle Onto** | crate, coach roof, ledge | AP cost | fail to mount |
| **Kick / Breach** | weak door/crate | AP cost | loud fail; wasted AP |
| **Take Cover** | cover cell edge | free: standing in a cover cell is cover | wrong side |

V1 does not need complex parkour chains or cinematic ledge systems.

**Swimming** (owner's decision, 2026-11-11, which replaces the earlier line that V1 does not need it): the character swims in deep water. Ordinary swimming is free, and a fast swim spends Stamina as the sprint does ([07.2](07.2%20Derived%20Character%20Statistics.md) §3.2); nobody drowns or dives, and an overloaded character swims at half speed. The Combat Grid has no cell in deep water, so no fight starts while the character swims, and beasts and enemies give up a chase at the water's edge. The depth and the speeds are the agent's authoring defaults.

*As built* (AN5, 2026-11-12; the agent's working readings under the owner's grant, the numbers authoring defaults unless marked measured; [ANIMATION_PIPELINE_PLAN.md](unity/Assets/Plans/ANIMATION_PIPELINE_PLAN.md) §12):
- *Deep water* is water 1.3 m or more over its bed, chest-deep on a person 1.8 m tall: walking in, the character swims once the bed falls that far, and falling in from a quay, once it has sunk that deep. It wades out where the bed rises to 1.15 m.
- *The swim* goes 1.8 m/s and the fast swim, with Shift, 3 m/s, spending Stamina as the sprint and stopping when Stamina runs out as the sprint does. Overloaded and Critical Overload ([07.2](07.2%20Derived%20Character%20Statistics.md) §5.2) halve both.
- *The body* floats with its root 0.05 m over the water, where the stroke holds the chest just under the surface and the head out of it (measured on the clip). It turns to where it swims, as the stroke goes forward only ([16](16%20Architecture%20Pivot.md) §2).
- *Climbing out*: Space at a bank whose top stands no more than 2.1 m over the water, with room to stand on it, climbs out in 0.8 s. The city's quays stand 1.9 m over the sea (measured), so a swimmer can leave the river and the harbour anywhere along them; elsewhere it wades out where the bed rises.
- *Fights and beasts*: no fight starts while the character swims or climbs out, a beast neither notices a swimmer nor keeps chasing one, and a pack that noticed the character gives up and goes home.

## Success and Failure

Each risky Environment Action has:

- a readable chance using the shared attribute/skill check foundation in [07.1 Character Attributes, Skills, and Action Checks](07.1%20Character%20Attributes,%20Skills,%20and%20Action%20Checks.md), where applicable, with the bands of § V1 numbers;
- a concrete fail result (Hurt, wasted Action, bad position);
- never empty “nothing happened” as the only fail for important attempts.

## V1 numbers

Authoring defaults set by the agent on 2026-11-03.

The check is the curve of [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md) with `Δ = attribute − D`. Climbing and Jumping are still candidate skills ([18 RPG Systems Coverage Draft](18%20RPG%20Systems%20Coverage%20Draft.md)), so V1 checks the attribute alone; a skill joins the average if it is taken in.

| Action | AP | Attribute | Secondary |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Vault / Jump | 2 | Dexterity | Strength |
| Climb Up, one level of up to 2 m | 3 | Strength | Endurance, Dexterity |
| Climb Through Window | 3 | Dexterity | Strength |
| Mantle Onto | 3 | Strength | Dexterity |
| Kick / Breach | 3 | Strength | Endurance |
| Open / Close | 1, a locked door cannot be opened in a fight without its key | — | — |
| Take Cover | 0: standing in a cover cell is cover | — | — |

The AP replace the 1 AP of the step across the tagged edge.

Each tag carries one of three bands:

| Band | D | Examples | Attribute 1 | Attribute 3 | Attribute 6 |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Easy | 1 | low wall, crate line, ground-floor window, weak door | 80 % | 95 % | 99 % |
| Hard | 3 | gap of two cells, ledge of 2 m, coach roof | 50 % | 80 % | 98 % |
| Very hard | 5 | gap of three cells, wall of 3 m, drainpipe, barred door | 20 % | 50 % | 90 % |

Secondary attributes and Luck adjust these as 07.1 says. No band reaches 100 %.

**Failure.** The AP are spent in every case.

- A failed vault or window leaves the character where it stood.
- A failed jump over a gap drops it to the level below.
- A failed climb or mantle drops it back.
- A fall takes 10 % of maximum HP for each 2 m fallen, which armour does not reduce, and ends the turn.
- A failed breach leaves the door shut.

So a failure costs time and position, and sometimes health, and is never empty.

Outside a fight free movement has no check: the grid and its bands exist only during Confront.

## Working readings in the slice (S3a.7)

The agent's readings of 2026-11-11, made under the owner's grant of autonomous work. They are authoring defaults, not owner decisions.

- **Where the tags stand.** The street railings and the field fences are tagged Vault / Jump, Easy. Other tags wait for the blocks that carry them: ledges, windows and weak doors in the kit get theirs when a fight needs them.
- **Where an action lands.** A vault or a window lands from 1.2 m below to 0.6 m above the cell it leaves. A climb lands 0.6–3.2 m above it, and a mantle 0.4–2.2 m above it. A vault over a crate line that leaves no room beside it lands one cell farther on.
- **Gaps.** A jump over a gap is not offered yet, because a gap has no block to carry its tag.
- **A failed climb or mantle** falls the whole height it rose.
- **A fall's damage** is proportional: 10 % of maximum HP for each 2 m, so 3 m takes 15 %.
- **Reaction Attacks.** A vault or a climb draws them only when it succeeds and the character leaves its cell.
- **A breach that works** clears its block from the place, and the grid reads the place again. A failed breach leaves the block standing.
- **Open / Close** waits: no fight door opens yet.
- **The cursor** offers an Environment Action over the cell past the tagged edge, when walking round would cost more AP or there is no way round. From Intelligence 10 the tip shows the action's chance, as it does a blow's. For a climb or a mantle the tip says how far a failure falls.

## Approach Mapping

| Approach | Environment use |
|----------|--------------------------|
| Veil | mantle / cover for concealment |
| Confront | breach, vault into melee |
| Withdraw / Break Away | jump gap / climb toward escape edge |
| Exchange / Convince | usually not traversal |
| Threaten | brandish from elevated mantle cell |

# Cover and Elevation

V1 soft rules:

- higher cell can grant simple advantage tell for Confront pressure;
- cover matters only against ranged attacks, magical or with firearms, because it is a problem of aiming: −15 points to hit behind Half Cover and −30 behind Full Cover (authoring defaults, [10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §5b); it adds to Obscured (*Shot Line*);
- in melee, no cover gives any advantage;
- a fence is **Half Cover**, a small hindrance to ranged attacks;
- trees are both obstacles and cover, by default **Full Cover**;
- World props (crates, coaches, pillars, trees) are the main toys.
- the grid reads cover on a thin strip beside each side of a cell, 0.3–0.9 m out and 0.6 m wide, a person's shoulders (since S3a.6, so a wall on one side no longer covers the two sides beside it), at a low and a high height, so a wall gives cover whatever its angle (authoring defaults, 2026-11-05; checked on streets at 0° and 45° in [VEILMOUTH_CITY_PLAN.md](unity/Assets/Plans/VEILMOUTH_CITY_PLAN.md) §12).

# Shot Line

Owner, 2026-11-13: the combat rework's concept §7 and the answer to Q10 (a) ([COMBAT_REWORK_PLAN.md](unity/Assets/Plans/COMBAT_REWORK_PLAN.md)). One rule for every projectile, a bullet or a magical one, in a fight and out of one, where the Aimed Shot uses it too ([10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §5e); never a second system for deciding hits. The values are authoring defaults checked by `tools/balance/tactical_sim.py` and `rework_sim.py`; the rest, where it says so, is the agent's working reading.

- **The line.** At the moment of an attack its line is traced from the muzzle, or the caster's hand, to three points of the target: the chest, the head, and the hips (working reading). The target can be chosen when at least one of them has a line free of solid obstacles, and the shot flies to the first free one, chest, head, hips in that order. A low fence therefore hides the hips and not the chest.
- **Solid obstacles** stop the line: walls, pillars, doors, carts, tree trunks, and every collider not marked partial. A target behind one cannot be chosen, and neither can an area ability's point.
- **Partial obstacles** are marked as such: bushes, hedges, low plants, haze, smoke, a tree's crown, and Пелена ([05.1](05.1%20Arcana%20and%20Limits.md)). A targeted attack whose line crosses one, the one the target stands in included, is **Obscured**: −20 to hit, once however many it crosses (COMBAT-4 in [99](99%20Open%20Questions.md)). A partial obstacle never stops a line.
- **Cover** stays as *Cover and Elevation* gives it, Half −15 and Full −30, and adds to Obscured.
- **Interception.** Each person standing on the line, nearest first, friend, enemy or bystander, takes the shot in 60 % of shots, before the target is rolled (the owner's "high chance"; the share is COMBAT-3). The shot is then rolled against whoever took it, with that person's own Evasion and cover. The fallen and the unconscious take none. Objects intercept by being solid.
- **A missed shot is lost.** It strikes nothing beyond (VS-ALARM (g)); the seen projectile flies to whoever it struck, or on, harmless.
- **An area ability** needs a line free of solid obstacles from the caster to its point. It then strikes everyone inside its area whose line from the point is free of solid obstacles, friends included, with no roll to hit and no critical hit. Partial obstacles do nothing to it.
- **On the grid** the people on a line are read from the cells it crosses; in the scene, from the colliders. Both read the same classified colliders, so a fight and the Aimed Shot agree.
- **In the slice** (CR2; working readings, COMBAT-12 in [99](99%20Open%20Questions.md)): the kit's bushes and hedges are its Partial Obstacles. Grass and flowers stand below the hips' point and the trees' crowns above every line on level ground, so neither is marked; haze, smoke and Пелена come with the abilities. A bush round the shooter does not make its own shot Obscured. A corner of a cell the line only touches puts nobody on it, and a person more than 1.5 m above or below the line stands on another floor. [10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §5f *In the slice* gives the build.

# Vertical Slice Implications

The slice must prove the Combat Grid in the city with:

- walkable grid over real geometry;
- cover from world props;
- one vault or mantle tag;
- a character leaving the fight by moving away.

Outside Confront, the overworld stub and interior scenes stay free-move.

# Solo Scope

## V1

- square grid truth **during Confront only**;
- pathfinding Move;
- small Environment Action set above;
- fail = Hurt / wasted Action / bad position.

## V2

- richer cover;
- more furniture verbs;
- more tagged world props.

## V3

- denser generated tags from seed;
- still reject freeform physics parkour as gameplay truth.

# Anti-Patterns

- treating overworld or interiors as grid-authored gameplay space outside Confront;
- building pretty places and bolting turns on later without cell tags;
- Environment Actions only in cutscenes;
- 0% fail fantasy jumps that delete Commitment;
- giving Confront unique traversal verbs denied to Veil/Withdraw;
- giant action list before the six Approaches feel good.
