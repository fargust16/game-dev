# Purpose

This document contains the three authored POI cards for the Vertical Slice in **Lower Veil District**.

Tone: industrial-occult 18–19th century transition. Placeholder names may change later.

Approach coverage across the three cards must include all six: Confront, Convince, Threaten, Exchange, Veil, Withdraw.

# District Framing for All Three

Common backdrop:

- gaslamps, soot, factory whistle at shift change;
- horse cabs on the cleaner ridge road; pushcarts and boots in Lower Veil;
- Wardens in dark coats and brass numbers;
- Veinmarket as whispered night-route / mixture trade, never announcing itself by true name in public;
- medicines scarce for the poor; richer districts buy first.

Shared Information Sources before the POIs:

- **Notice Board** outside the shift tavern;
- **Bartender** at the Soot & Bell;
- **Street Rumour** near the factory fence;
- later **Physical Trace** for POI C.

# POI A — Brass-Number Arrest at Harrow & Tincture

```text
## Decision Node: Brass-Number Arrest at Harrow & Tincture

Status: Slice-ready draft
World Seed sensitivity: Low for Vertical Slice (fixed prototype seed)
Slice IDs: POI-A / ApothecaryArrest

### POI packaging
POI type: Street Conflict
Map presentation: Hinted from board/rumour; Marked after bartender tip or reading the pinned summons
Discovery sources:
- Notice Board: "Warden seizure notice — Harrow & Tincture, before second-shift bell"
- Bartender: "Brass-numbers are taking old Harrow tonight. Say he sold black tincture to mill hands."
- Street Rumour: "They want the drawers more than the man."
- Stumble: arrive during the arrest on Tincture Row
Interaction radius note: shop front + first three paces of the cobbles; entering starts the node
Active Window class: Short
  starts after afternoon board posting;
  ends at second-shift bell / night factory whistle (same evening)
How the player learns urgency:
- board text names the second-shift bell;
- bartender: "If you care, go before the whistle. After that it's paperwork and padlocks."
- Marked map uses Short urgency
Unattended resolution summary:
- if Wardens stronger locally: Harrow taken, shop sealed with wax and brass seal
- if Veinmarket pressure stronger: Harrow vanishes out the back, drawers emptied, broken glass
Aftermath Symptom if missed:
- sealed shutters / chalk cross on door / medicine prices jump for mill families / denser Warden pairs on Tincture Row
Follow-up POI chance:
- always can feed POI C (Aftermath Trace) with either seized-drawer route or back-alley powder trail

### 1. Situation
Gaslight sputters over Tincture Row. Two Wardens with brass collar-numbers try to drag Apothecary Harrow from his narrow shop.
A third Warden blocks the door, eyeing a locked oak cabinet.
Mill neighbours shout that Harrow sells cheap cough draughts when the factory fog burns their lungs.
Harrow swears the "black tincture" talk is a frame.
The player arrives while the street can still tip either way.

### 2. Active Decision Axes
Primary: Allegiance
Secondary: Approach
Optional third: Attention / Commitment (leave before the whistle)

### 3. What the player knows
Observable facts:
- arrest happening now;
- Wardens care about the locked cabinet;
- neighbours depend on cheap medicine;
- window ends at the second-shift whistle.

Symptoms / rumours / clues:
- factory cough is worse this month;
- Veinmarket whisper about "night doses";
- Wardens have been sealing more shops than usual.

Relevant Player Knowledge:
- Wardens often speak in summons language;
- if player started as Veinmarket, contacts may already distrust brass-numbers;
- Veil before choosing can reveal who sent the third Warden.

### 4. What stays hidden
Hidden Influence:
- small shift toward Wardens or Veinmarket depending on outcome;
- whether Harrow quietly supplies Veinmarket night routes.

Hidden delayed consequences:
- mentor access (Warden drill or Veinmarket mixture tip);
- POI C flavour: wax-seal ledger vs alley powder.

Discoverable with effort:
- Veil: overhear the third Warden ask for "the crowned buyer's list" not the man;
- Convince neighbour: Harrow refused a factory foreman's private order yesterday;
- after access: cabinet holds labeled vials and one unmarked black bottle.

### 5. Options

Option name: Defend Harrow
Axes: Allegiance + Approach
Approaches: Confront and/or Convince (distract/argue), Threaten if pressuring Wardens to back off
Immediate cost: Warden heat, possible Turn-Based Confront, fine, injury
Readable consequence: shop stays open tonight; Harrow owes a debt
Opens: cheap medicine, later mixture clue, Veinmarket soft favour if player already leans that way
Closes: easy Warden mentorship; clean legal cover
Failure: cabinet still seized or cracked; player learns the list mattered more than Harrow

Option name: Help the Wardens seize him
Axes: Allegiance + Approach
Approaches: Confront, Threaten, Convince (testify), Exchange (offer to "hold the crowd")
Immediate cost: neighbour hostility; Veinmarket suspicion
Readable consequence: brass seal on the door; Wardens mark player useful
Opens: Warden tip about night patrols; temporary legal cover; drill mentor stub
Closes: Harrow aid; soft Veinmarket doors
Failure: Harrow flees with the black bottle; player still hears "crowned buyer" slip

Option name: Veil and listen first
Axes: Attention + Approach
Approach: Veil (Turn-Based Mode outside combat recommended for approach)
Immediate cost: spends Short window time; may miss clean public save
Readable consequence: hears crowned-buyer / list fragment
Opens: better Exchange leverage; Yield Threshold edge; smarter POI C start
Closes: innocent-hero timing with the crowd
Failure: spotted; both sides colder; still get one partial tell

Option name: Buy a minute / Exchange with someone
Axes: Commitment + Approach
Approach: Exchange
  - pay a neighbour to jam the lane;
  - or slip a Warden a "finding fee" for looking at the cabinet later
Immediate cost: scarce coin / favour
Readable consequence: temporary delay or partial access without full Allegiance lock-in
Opens: hybrid path into defend or seize with more info
Closes: looking neutral forever; someone remembers the bribe
Failure: money lost; whistle closer; scene hardens into Confront

Option name: Walk away before the whistle
Axes: Attention + Commitment
Approach: Withdraw
Immediate cost: miss live rewards
Readable consequence: unattended resolution + morning Symptoms
Opens: cleaner entry into POI C as investigator of aftermath
Closes: Harrow debt and Warden favour
Failure: not a reload fail; world teaches sealed-shop language

### 6. World residue
Influence: hidden nudge to Wardens or Veinmarket
Symptoms: open/sealed shop, medicine access, patrol pairs, rumour tone at Soot & Bell
Paths: medicine ally vs Warden mentor pressured against each other
Legacy note: out of slice scope, but sealed wax / broken glass remain as readable set dressing for POI C

### 7. Curiosity check
Hypotheses:
- "If Harrow is only a kind draught-seller, why the cabinet?"
- "If Wardens keep order for the poor, why do mill families hate this seizure?"
Remaining question:
- "Who is the crowned buyer?"

### 8. Validation
Curiosity? Yes
Teaches world? Yes
Priced power? Yes if later aid/tools come with debt or heat
Multiple viable options? Yes
Failure valuable? Yes
World different afterward? Yes
Lazy/timed POI? Yes
Era mood? Yes (gaslight, brass numbers, factory whistle, mill cough)
```

# POI B — Eleventh-Bell Collectors

```text
## Decision Node: Eleventh-Bell Collectors

Status: Slice-ready draft
World Seed sensitivity: Low for Vertical Slice
Slice IDs: POI-B / NightAbductions

### POI packaging
POI type: Street Conflict
Map presentation: Hinted by rumour; Marked after bartender warning
Discovery sources:
- Bartender: "After the eleventh bell they take mill girls off Loom Lane. Tonight again."
- Notice Board: missing-person paper, edges greased by soot thumbs
- Street Rumour: "Collectors in coach-coats. Not proper cabmen."
- Stumble: enter Loom Lane during the window
Interaction radius note: Loom Lane bend + alley mouth toward the coal stairs
Active Window class: Brief
  from eleventh bell to about 02:00 local
How the player learns urgency:
- bartender names eleventh bell and "tonight";
- Marked map uses Brief urgency;
- if player waits, morning Aftermath replaces the live scene
Unattended resolution summary:
- Veinmarket-leaning miss: girls taken into a shuttered side-coach; glove/powder left
- Warden-leaning miss: patrol spoils the snatch late; one abductor dead, trail colder, victims still harmed or scattered
Aftermath Symptom if missed:
- shuttered windows, fewer women on early shift, chalk marks near coal stairs, "collector" whisper
Follow-up POI chance:
- feeds POI C with coach-track / coal-stair / glove Trace

### 1. Situation
After the eleventh bell, Loom Lane empties faster than it should.
A side-coach without crest lamps waits past the coal stairs.
Two figures in coach-coats drag a screaming mill girl toward the alley; another girl fights free for one breath.
A distant Warden whistle is too far.
The player can still stop them, follow them, signal for brass-numbers, or leave the lane to its night.

### 2. Active Decision Axes
Primary: Allegiance
Secondary: Approach
Optional third: Attention / Commitment (spend the Brief window on trail vs rescue)

### 3. What the player knows
Observable facts:
- abduction happening now;
- coach waits;
- Brief window;
- rescue and pursuit are both possible.

Symptoms / rumours:
- missing girls this week;
- Veinmarket night routes;
- Wardens claim they "control the lanes" but arrive late.

Relevant Player Knowledge:
- Open Path lean may prefer open stop;
- Veiled Path lean may prefer trail;
- Turn-Based Mode helps Veil approach along the coal stairs.

### 4. What stays hidden
Hidden Influence:
- whether collectors serve Veinmarket directly or sell to a richer "crowned" demand linked to POI A
Hidden delayed:
- ritual / labour / blackmail purpose fragment revealed only through trail or Yield
Discoverable:
- Veil: coach has no cab license plate; inside smells of chemical sweet tincture
- Threaten/Yield: one collector offers a coal-stair destination for his life
- after rescue: girl mentions "they asked who bought black cough from Harrow"

### 5. Options

Option name: Protect the victims now
Axes: Allegiance + Approach
Approaches: Confront (likely Turn-Based), Convince bystanders to swarm, Threaten collectors
Immediate cost: injury/death risk; noise; maybe killing the best informant
Readable consequence: one or more girls free tonight
Opens: gratitude (seeded: coal-stair key whisper / small fund / named fear of "crowned coach")
Closes: clean trail to destination; some collectors escape with the coach
Failure: partial rescue; still recover glove or chemical sweet smell as Trace seed
Break Away / Spare: available in Confront; Spare can buy destination fragment

Option name: Follow the coach instead
Axes: Allegiance + Approach + Commitment
Approach: Veil (manual Turn-Based Mode strongly useful)
Immediate cost: victims remain in danger; moral weight; spotted risk
Readable consequence: learn coal-stair landing or warehouse shutters
Opens: strong POI C / possible later interruption setup
Closes: rescue gratitude; some victims lost before any later act
Failure: spotted → forced Confront in worse position; or lose coach but see Veinmarket chalk mark

Option name: Signal or fetch Wardens
Axes: Allegiance + Approach
Approaches: Convince / Threaten urgency; Exchange if promising a tip to brass-numbers
Immediate cost: time; response may be late or brutal; Veinmarket enmity
Readable consequence: official version written into the lane
Opens: Warden favour; report access
Closes: underworld informants; rescued girls may distrust player who brought batons
Failure: Wardens botch it; player sees if this post is corrupt, scared, or simply late

Option name: Exchange for a tip before moving
Axes: Commitment + Approach
Approach: Exchange with a door-watching crone / cab-boy for "which stair they always use"
Immediate cost: coin/favour; spends Brief minutes
Readable consequence: better Veil route or ambush corner
Opens: smarter rescue or follow
Closes: tip-seller may sell you out later
Failure: bad tip into a dead yard; coach already rolling

Option name: Leave Loom Lane
Axes: Attention + Commitment
Approach: Withdraw
Immediate cost: miss live scene
Readable consequence: morning Symptoms + colder Trace
Opens: investigator angle into POI C without blood on hands
Closes: rescue debts and live destination
Failure: world shows what "collector night" looks like after

### 6. World residue
Influence: hidden nudge toward Veinmarket sponsors or Warden "order restored" fiction
Symptoms: night fear, shift attendance, chalk, coach gossip at Soot & Bell
Paths: people-debt path vs sponsor-trail path
Link to POI A: rescued girl / Yield confession may connect black tincture buyers to crowned demand

### 7. Curiosity check
Hypotheses:
- "Why living captives, not corpses?"
- "Is this the same crowned appetite that wanted Harrow's cabinet?"
Remaining question:
- "What is being collected for?"

### 8. Validation
All Decision Engine + POI checks pass.
Era mood: coach-coats, coal stairs, mill girls, eleventh bell, brass whistle far away.
```

# POI C — Soot Stair Trace

```text
## Decision Node: Soot Stair Trace

Status: Slice-ready draft
World Seed sensitivity: Low for Vertical Slice
Slice IDs: POI-C / AftermathTrace
Appears after: POI A and/or POI B has resolved (participated or missed)

### POI packaging
POI type: Trace / Clue
Map presentation: Hinted by bartender/rumour after aftermath; Marked when player accepts the lead or finds the first Physical Trace
Discovery sources:
- Bartender next day: "Lane's wrong. Coal stairs got fresh chalk."
- Street Rumour: "Wax seal melted funny on Harrow's door" OR "glove by the coach ruts"
- Physical Trace: depending on prior residue
  - from A: brass-wax scrap / torn buyer list corner / black-bottle glass
  - from B: coach-rut oil, chemical-sweet rag, woman's glove, Veinmarket chalk
Interaction radius note: coal stair landing under the factory wall; optional second room = shuttered inspection nook
Active Window class: Standard
  lasts a couple of in-world days after spawn
How the player learns urgency:
- bartender: "Rain'll wash the chalk by midweek."
- Marked map uses Standard urgency, calmer than Brief
Unattended resolution summary:
- if ignored: chalk fades; richer cleaners or Veinmarket sweepers remove the easy tells
- world still shows a weaker Symptom (less evidence, colder trail)
Aftermath Symptom if missed:
- cleaner stairs, missing glove, rumour dies into shrug
Follow-up POI chance:
- Vertical Slice ends after this node's Bargain tease; no fourth POI required
- may foreshadow future content beyond slice

### 1. Situation
Beneath the factory wall, the coal stairs smell of soot and sweet chemical.
Something was moved here after the arrest night and/or the collector night.
The player can read the Trace carefully, ask a witness, buy a sweep's silence, sneak into the shuttered nook, or leave the stairs to the rain.

This node also carries the slice's **Bargain tease**:
In the nook (or offered by a polite stranger in a too-clean coat) lies a folded note and a single black drop sealed in glass:
"Drink, and the fog will speak your enemies' steps. Refuse, and remain ordinary lungs in an ordinary choke."

### 2. Active Decision Axes
Primary: Attention
Secondary: Approach
Optional third: Bargain (accept/refuse the black drop) and Commitment (time before rain)

### 3. What the player knows
Observable facts:
- Trace site exists because previous residue;
- rain/time will degrade evidence;
- a stranger or note offers priced power.

Clues vary by prior POI outcomes:
- if A helped Wardens: wax/brass language stronger;
- if A helped Harrow: bottle glass / Veinmarket chalk stronger;
- if B rescued: gratitude tip points here;
- if B followed: player may already know the landing and arrive with advantage;
- if either missed: thinner evidence, still enough for one true tell.

### 4. What stays hidden
- full identity of the crowned buyer;
- whether the black drop is church, entity, or Veinmarket-adjacent External Power;
- long-term distortion price (only foreshadowed).

Discoverable:
- Convince a soot-covered sweep who saw a crestless coach;
- Veil into the nook without alerting a watcher;
- Exchange for the torn list corner;
- Threaten a looter picking the glove;
- Confront only if a watcher turns violent (should be avoidable).

### 5. Options

Option name: Study the Physical Trace
Axes: Attention + Approach
Approach: Veil / careful Interact (Turn-Based Mode outside combat useful)
Immediate cost: time; possible notice by watcher
Readable consequence: one solid tell linking A and B (tincture + collectors)
Opens: clearer crowned-buyer hypothesis; better Bargain judgment
Closes: none major
Failure: misread once, but still notice chemical-sweet + soot together

Option name: Talk to the sweep / witness
Axes: Attention + Approach
Approaches: Convince, Threaten, or Exchange
Immediate cost: coin, favour, or enmity
Readable consequence: "crestless coach" + "spoke like upridge clerks"
Opens: social confirmation of class divide behind the crime
Closes: if Threaten, sweep vanishes from future tips
Failure: silence; player still has environmental Trace

Option name: Enter the shuttered nook
Axes: Attention + Approach (+ Bargain when note is found)
Approaches: Veil to enter quietly, Confront if forced, Withdraw if aborting
Immediate cost: trespass heat; Bargain temptation
Readable consequence: find note + black drop; optional confrontation with polite watcher
Opens: Bargain tease resolution
Closes: clean innocence with Wardens if caught trespassing
Failure: caught; Watcher flees leaving only the note

Option name: Accept the black drop
Axes: Bargain + Commitment
Approach: Bargain (may use Action in TB if tense)
Immediate cost: unknown/delayed price; foreshadow breath/hearing distortion
Readable consequence: minor "fog sense" stub — player notices patrol/collector tension more easily for the rest of the slice
Opens: priced power fantasy proof
Closes: refusal purity; later Soft Memory hooks out of slice scope
Failure: if interrupted mid-accept, spill creates a harsher Symptom and no benefit

Option name: Refuse the black drop
Axes: Bargain + Attention
Approach: Withdraw from the Bargain (can still take the note as info)
Immediate cost: no power stub
Readable consequence: keep the warning text as Knowledge; leave the drop or smash it
Opens: moral/knowledge path; possible later safer research beyond slice
Closes: fog-sense stub
Failure: none; refusal is valid success of judgment

Option name: Leave the stairs to the rain
Axes: Attention + Commitment
Approach: Withdraw
Immediate cost: evidence decay
Readable consequence: weaker world tell; slice can still end if Bargain was already resolved or skipped
Opens: acceptance that not every Trace must be finished
Closes: best A+B link
Failure: curiosity unpaid; still proves timed world if chalk is gone next visit

### 6. World residue
- chalk gone or deepened;
- if drop accepted: player bears a tiny readable side effect (e.g. street gaslight hum feels sharper; one dialogue option gets odd);
- if smashed: sweet chemical stain remains on stone;
- Soot & Bell rumour updates with whichever tell the player proved.

### 7. Curiosity check
Hypotheses:
- "The poor district is being harvested for someone upridge."
- "Order and night trade may both feed the same appetite."
Remaining question for beyond slice:
- "What does the fog want spoken — and what will it take later?"

### 8. Validation
Curiosity? Yes
Teaches world? Yes (class divide + linked crimes)
Priced power? Yes (black drop Bargain)
Multiple viable options? Yes
Failure valuable? Yes
World different afterward? Yes
Lazy/timed POI? Yes
Era mood? Yes (coal stairs, crestless coach, upridge clerks, factory wall)
Approaches used here: Veil, Convince, Threaten, Exchange, Confront optional, Withdraw, plus Bargain
```

# Slice Linkage Rules

1. POI C spawns after the first of A/B resolves, and updates if the second also resolves before the player finishes C.
2. Evidence density in C scales with participation quality, but never to zero if at least one of A/B resolved unattended.
3. The "crowned buyer / crestless coach / upridge clerk" thread is the curiosity spine tying medicine seizure to night collection.
4. No fourth POI is required for slice completion after C's Bargain accept/refuse (or deliberate Withdraw after reading the note).
