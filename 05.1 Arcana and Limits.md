# Purpose

This document holds the structure of supernatural advancement as content: which Arcana exist, what each Limit is called, which attributes each Arcana asks for, and what a Limit grants. The rules of advancement stay in [05 Progression](05%20Progression.md).

Limit names and attribute profiles are the owner's tables, kept in Russian as supplied.

# Terms

- An **Arcana** is one of the 22 Major Arcana archetypes of the Tarot.
- A **Limit** is one of the ten steps within an Arcana, numbered 9 to 0.
- Each Limit corresponds to one of the ten **Sephiroth** of Kabbalah, from Malkuth at Limit 9 to Keter at Limit 0.
- Each Limit of each Arcana has its own name.

A character is described by these three: for example, Шут, Limit 9, Бродяга. The project has no separate paths and no path names.

# What a Limit grants

Owner-approved:

- **An Ability Pool for each Limit** (owner, 2026-11-13, the combat rework's answer to Q5 (b)): a Limit opens a pool of 4–6 abilities; the character picks some of them on reaching it and learns the rest later, from teachers, books, Bargains or artefacts. This replaces the owner's earlier rule of exactly two abilities a Limit. Which are prepared for a fight is set by the Prepared Abilities of [07.2](07.2%20Derived%20Character%20Statistics.md) §4.5, so the pool widens the style and the slots limit a fight.
- Abilities can be passive or active, and combat or exploration. The mix differs by Arcana. Passive abilities always work and take no Prepared slot.
- Abilities accumulate, so a character at Limit 7 knows its picks of three pools.
- Will Power is the only limit of an ability's use, with no cooldown ([07.2](07.2%20Derived%20Character%20Statistics.md) §3.3). A targeted ability aims by Intelligence and the Arcana Skill, never Will, and no attribute adds to its damage ([10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §5f).
- NPCs use exactly the same abilities as the player for their Arcana and Limit.
- Each Limit also raises the Arcana's primary attributes.

A Limit raises the Arcana's attributes by an amount that grows with depth, so the step between two Limits is felt in attributes as well as in abilities. Owner-approved on 2026-11-02:

| Target Limit | 9 | 8 | 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Each primary attribute | +3 | +4 | +5 | +6 | +7 | +8 | +8 | +9 | +9 | +10 |
| Secondary attribute | +1 | +2 | +2 | +2 | +2 | +3 | +3 | +3 | +3 | +3 |

The curve replaces the first direction of about 30 % of the target Limit's band, which taken literally totals 165 on a 1–100 scale and removes training from Limit 5.

Working reading: the ten attribute bands of [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md) map onto the ten Limits, with Limit 9 on 1–9 and Limit 0 on 90–99. A character who enters each Limit at the bottom of its band then needs 6 points of training early on and 1 near the end, so training always counts.

| Cult | Императрица | Occupied by a sane god |
| Cult | Иерофант | Occupied by a sane god |

The two sane gods of the cult want to use the True God's descent into the world for their own ends. This is the current direction and may change.

Under the Limit Occupancy Rule of [05 Progression](05%20Progression.md), the Жрица, Император, Императрица, and Иерофант Arcana have no Limit 1, while the Шут and Маг Arcana can have up to three Limit 1 practitioners. All three cult Arcana stop at Limit 2, so the three Cult Generals are the top of the cult's living hierarchy.

The sealed being's Limit 0 is Престол. The Spirit General stands at Limit 2 of the same Arcana.

The other 16 Arcana have no faction yet. They are prepared after the Vertical Slice is proven.

# Vertical Slice

| Arcana | Style of play | How it is reached |
|---|---|---|
| Жрица | Dialogue and stealth | The internal-order faction offers it |
| Император | Confrontation | The character finds it alone |

The church has no next Limit for Император, so a character on it must seek the next Limit among the cultists. Both Arcana have an occupied Limit 0, so the player starts on a fork that can never reach Limit 0.

In the slice, cult followers use only Император, and allied followers use only Жрица. These 16 abilities therefore cover both sides. Императрица and Иерофант wait for the full game.

Five of Жрица now beat five of Император 53 % at Limit 8 and 46 % at Limit 7, and the slice, whose allies are all Жрица and whose cult is all Император, falls to 20 %, and after the firearms pass of the same day stands at 23 %, which the owner accepted ([10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §5b). With the firearms pass of the same day a rifle shot costs 6 AP, and Укол разума costs 4 AP in place of 5 so that it still fits beside one in a turn of 10 AP (authoring default). With the combat rework of 2026-11-13 no attribute adds to an ability's damage, so Укол разума's base rises from 7 to 9, taking in the old Intelligence factor of a Limit 7 caster; it aims by Intelligence and the Arcana Skill, and Дрёма's and Стой!'s Will Contests read the caster's Intelligence and Arcana Skill in place of its higher of Intelligence and Will ([10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §5f). The four new abilities' numbers are authoring defaults not yet run in a model (the asterisk); Гнёт's base of 4 makes one cast about one pistol shot's harm to each person it strikes, with no roll.

## Conduct Progress in the slice

Authoring defaults set by the agent on 2026-11-03, under the owner's direction that the agent sets the slice's numbers. They follow the V1 rule of [05 Progression](05%20Progression.md): simple, readable, and never one action repeated forever.

- A **deed** is an action that fits the Arcana. Each deed is worth 1 point, and 2 when it settles the Decision Node of a POI. Only deeds done after reaching Limit 9 count.
- **Limit 8 needs 8 points.**
- Each kind of deed counts at most three times, and each deed needs a different person or place. Four kinds at three each give 12, so the threshold needs at least three kinds.
- An act against the Arcana takes 1 point away, never below zero.
- No counter is shown. After a deed the character feels it in one short line, and when the threshold is met it feels ready for the next potion.

| Arcana | Kinds of deed | Against the Arcana |
|---|---|---|
| Жрица | Win a Convince check, with Внушение or without it; overhear a conversation unseen; go through a watched or forbidden place without filling a Notice Meter; take something unseen, by theft or from a watched place | Starting a fight when a way without one was open |
| Император | Win a fight against a side at least as strong; win a Threaten check, Стой! outside a fight included; beat a stronger single enemy, a deeper Limit or a glowing beast; stand when a way out was open: refuse Break Away, a Yield, or a hidden route, and finish the fight | A stealth attack, a theft, Break Away, or a Yield |

**The run's budget.** POIs A, B, and C settled in the Arcana's way give 2 each, so 6, and two or three small deeds in the city or outside it give the rest. A player who aims at Limit 8 reaches it on the second day, and one who only follows the story collects about 4, which leaves Limit 8 the directed effort that [11 Vertical Slice](11%20Vertical%20Slice.md) asks for. POI A closes on the first evening, so a player who drinks the Limit 9 potion after it must find other deeds.

**The potions' ingredients.** The Limit 9 potion takes ordinary herbs and one magical ingredient that the Master of Enchanting sells. The Limit 8 potion takes herbs and a magical ingredient from a glowing beast outside the city, which the same Master sells at a high price. The second potion therefore asks for a trip outside the city or a great deal of money.

**Requirements and Sanity in the slice.** Authoring defaults set by the agent on 2026-11-04 under the formula of [05 Progression](05%20Progression.md): `Sanity_after = Sanity_before − Mandatory_Transition_Cost × Brewing_Factor − Preparation_Penalty`.

| Target Limit | Each primary attribute | Secondary attribute | Mandatory Sanity cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 3 | 2 | 10 |
| 8 | 7 | 4 | 15 |

- Each point a primary attribute falls short costs 2 Sanity, and each point the secondary falls short costs 1.
- Requirements are read before drinking, so the rise that Limit 9 gives counts toward Limit 8.
- The Brewing Factor of [20](20%20Alchemy%20and%20Potioncraft.md) §3 multiplies the mandatory cost only, never the preparation penalty.
- In the slice the character keeps its sanity unless Sanity reaches 0, which ends the run ([07.2](07.2%20Derived%20Character%20Statistics.md)).

Worked examples from Sanity 100, with good brewing:

| Character | Limit 9 | Limit 8 | Sanity after both |
|---|---|---|---|
| Сыщик on Жрица (Will short) | 10 + 1 = 11 | 15 + 2 = 17 | about 72 |
| Боец on Император (Will short) | 10 + 4 = 14 | 15 + 6 = 21 | about 65 |

The worst case, an untrained character on Император with every brewing result bad (×2.5), loses about 85 and ends near 15, deep in the penalties of [07.2](07.2%20Derived%20Character%20Statistics.md) but alive. Potions alone therefore never end a run; they only add to what exhaustion and the world take.

## Failure and exhaustion

- An active ability spends its Will Power whether or not it succeeds.
- An ability aimed at another person is resisted, and failure has its own consequence, listed above. Failure is never just wasted time.
- An ability aimed at the character has no check. Its price is a drawback, listed above.
- A passive ability costs nothing in use. Its price was paid in Sanity on advancing to its Limit.
- Reaching zero Will Power is full exhaustion, which takes a little Sanity ([07.2](07.2%20Derived%20Character%20Statistics.md)).
