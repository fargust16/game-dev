# Purpose

This document holds the structure of supernatural advancement as content: which Arcana exist, what each Limit is called, which attributes each Arcana asks for, and what a Limit grants. The rules of advancement stay in [05 Progression](05%20Progression.md).

Limit names and attribute profiles are the owner's tables, kept in Russian as supplied.

# Terms

- An **Arcana** is one of the 22 Major Arcana archetypes of the Tarot. It replaces the word Pathway.
- A **Limit** is one of the ten steps within an Arcana, numbered 9 to 0 as before. It replaces the word Stage for supernatural advancement.
- Each Limit corresponds to one of the ten **Sephiroth** of Kabbalah, from Malkuth at Limit 9 to Keter at Limit 0.
- Each Limit of each Arcana has its own name, and that name is used for the character's Limit.

Older documents still say Pathway and Stage. Read them as Arcana and Limit until the rename is done (CC-30). This applies only to supernatural advancement: terrain stages, item stages, and task-difficulty stages are unrelated.

# What a Limit grants

Owner-approved:

- Each Limit grants **two abilities**, from Limit 9 to Limit 0. An Arcana therefore has 20. This count is fixed for the Vertical Slice and may change later.
- Abilities can be passive or active, and combat or exploration. The mix differs by Arcana. Passive abilities count among the two.
- Abilities accumulate, so a character at Limit 7 has about six.
- NPCs use exactly the same abilities as the player for their Arcana and Limit.
- Each Limit also raises the Arcana's primary attributes.

How much a Limit raises them, and the abilities themselves, are open (CC-29).

| Side | Arcana | Limit 0 |
|---|---|---|

| Internal-order faction | Шут | Free |
| Internal-order faction | Маг | Free |
| Cult | Император | Occupied by the sealed being |
| Cult | Императрица | Open (CC-22) |
| Cult | Иерофант | Open (CC-22) |

Under the Stage Occupancy Rule of [05 Progression](05%20Progression.md), the Жрица and Император Arcana have no Limit 1, while the Шут and Маг Arcana can have up to three Limit 1 practitioners.

The sealed being's Limit 0 is Престол. The Spirit General stands at Limit 2 of the same Arcana, Устав.

Earlier working names for these six were Path of Change (Шут), Path of the Creator (Маг), Path of the Veil (Жрица), Path of the Source (Императрица), Path of the Sovereign (Император), and Path of Revelation (Иерофант). Whether they stay alongside the Arcana names is open (CC-30).

The other 16 Arcana have no faction yet. They are prepared after the Vertical Slice is proven.

# Vertical Slice

- The slice offers one or two potions, so that a run has two different styles of play: one through dialogue and stealth, the other through confrontation.
- A character outside the faction can find potion recipes too, including the cult's, with a somewhat different approach.

Which Arcana they are is open (CC-29).

# Known table issues

These come from checking the two tables against each other and against the project's terms (CC-31):

- Five Limit names repeat: Мера (Справедливость 9, Умеренность 5), Граница (Справедливость 7, Мир 4), Закон (Император 6, Колесо 2), Прозрение (Башня 5, Отшельник 2), Откровение (Солнце 2, Повешенный 1).
- Three Limit names match another term: Предел (Колесница 0) matches the word Limit, Воля (Маг 5) matches the attribute, and Суд (Император 4) matches an Arcana.
- The Arcana Сила has the same name as the Strength attribute.
- Four attribute profiles are identical: Маг and Умеренность; Жрица, Луна, and Суд; Иерофант and Справедливость; Влюблённые and Звезда.
- Will is primary for 14 of 22 Arcana, Dexterity for 3, and Strength for 1.
