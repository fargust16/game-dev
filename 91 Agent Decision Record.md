# Accepted Draft Decisions

## Campaign and world threat

- Owner-approved faction and world-threat direction (2026-10-31; campaign concept extended 2026-11-01): three cooperating church-associated Major Factions form one side, with internal-order, industry/artifact research, and external/maritime-security roles. The opposing Major Faction seeks to return an insane, sealed Limit 0 being called the True God or False God by followers. Ancient cosmic powers are the setting-level origin of supernatural power and seek to reclaim it through this being. If unopposed, it destroys other powerful beings and factions, people, its own followers, and ultimately the planet through a later act distinct from awakening. Before manifestation, the campaign is a war for cities under [21 Cult Campaign and City Control](21%20Cult%20Campaign%20and%20City%20Control.md): at world creation every city is allied; three Limit 2 Cult Generals (working roles Spirit, Holding, Raiding) capture cities by rituals through Cult Sorties, launched when generation has given a General enough followers, never from a calendar; City Capture clears every follower of other factions and leaves a garrison, and a one-hour ritual runs alongside it, conducted at a guarded site; allies beat the main force first and go to the ritual afterwards, only the player can choose to go for it first (the capital's final battle is the one exception, owner-approved on 2026-11-02), and a Cult Retreat ends it; a defender of Limit 5 or deeper who knows the method undoes a completed ritual within 60 ticks of reaching the site; a ritual stops when its conductors or its senior die, or, only while the city is not fully taken, through a Ritual Weak Point, which differs each time; a new ritual in a city waits two game days, and a liberated Place of Power lapses a game day later unless its ritual is undone sooner; it never attacks ordinary residents, who are kept alive as sacrifices in a Place of Power, a purpose nobody in the world knows and the player learns only at manifestation, with no dialogue to convince NPCs in later worlds; City Liberation is symmetrical, a liberated city goes to the allied faction with most followers there or stays Unclaimed until an allied follower arrives; every group acts on a Force Estimate; cult followers retreat collectively only when it is very strongly against them, fight on when retreat has no chance either, never hide, and share the Cult Thought Link; captured cities are closed combat zones where residents and shops keep working and stealth is possible; the Capital Gate lets the final ritual begin only when every other city is under Cult Control, and manifestation succeeds only if every city was captured at the moment of that ritual and it was not interrupted. The campaign lasts 15–20 game days at the middle difficulty, about 40–50 real hours; the target world is 3×3 tiles (4200 × 4200 m) with 5–7 cities, ideally 7. Only cities are cult targets. Every Limit 0 being has been on the unreachable Divine Plane since a past cataclysm and needs a very great number of sacrifices to materialise; the allied gods refused that price. The Spirit World is a separate place that higher followers can reach and the player cannot for now. Under the Limit Occupancy Rule ([05 Progression](05%20Progression.md)), on every Arcana, the player's included, an occupied Limit 0 leaves no Limit 1, and an unoccupied one allows at most three, so a god's own Arcana stops at Limit 2; all three cult Arcana are occupied, so the Generals are the top of the cult's living hierarchy. Allied forces are generated at world creation and replaced slowly; Cult Forces generate much faster and are not exhausted by killing rank-and-file followers until the cult's leadership is destroyed; the eighth slider, Cult Pace, owns their pace; a full city holds about 20 followers. At the lowest setting allied forces can kill all three Generals without the player. Generated cult followers still use the canonical character formulas. The player receives a private daily summary of their own actions, while public events reach the character through daily news, rumours, notes, witnesses, traces, assignments, ambient NPC reports, and allied briefings. There is no global ritual counter; reported City Control is the public reading of campaign progress. Each ritual lasts one in-game hour (60 ticks) without a player-facing numeric countdown. All three allied Major Factions receive advance intelligence about attacks and send groups to defend the city; those clashes resolve without the player, who may join or ignore them. Rituals can run in parallel in two cities when the cult has enough followers. Once the Capital Gate is open, the final awakening ritual occurs at the fixed centre of the capital; completion manifests the being there. The being remains at the capital, and the cult and allies contest the capital while cult forces move against any liberated city. The player can finish the campaign before manifestation only by killing all three Cult Generals, or after manifestation by defeating the being; both paths present a rating. Qualitative final-battle direction: allied forces are expected to win at the lowest Combat Difficulty, Normal is near-even with a slight enemy advantage, and higher settings increasingly favour the enemy; exact values and other-slider effects remain open. After manifestation, the being cannot be delayed or contained, and player death is defeat with no same-world successor even if the being is later defeated. Ritual concurrency, off-screen resolution, capture, later planet-destruction trigger, final-fight composition, rating criteria, and post-campaign play remain open. The mind-controlled ideal-world ending is withdrawn. A royal institution is influenced by the antagonist, but its role remains open. One state is playable; neighbouring states are background only. Limit 0 belief/anchor/sanity mechanics are out of scope. Keep unresolved mechanics and scope questions in [02 World Laws](02%20World%20Laws.md), [21 Cult Campaign and City Control](21%20Cult%20Campaign%20and%20City%20Control.md), and [99 Open Questions](99%20Open%20Questions.md); the document's Working readings are not owner decisions until confirmed.

## Progression

- Progression has no XP level-up. The game has 22 Arcana, and each has ten Limits tied to the Sephiroth ([05.1 Arcana and Limits](05.1%20Arcana%20and%20Limits.md)). Each Limit grants two abilities, shared by players and NPCs, and raises the Arcana's attributes on a fixed curve. V1 offers two player Arcana with different styles of play (Жрица from the faction, Император found alone), with cult followers only on Император and allies only on Жрица, and their 12 abilities in [05.1](05.1%20Arcana%20and%20Limits.md), player Limits 9→8 with Limit 7 for NPCs only, one joinable faction plus Bargain/quest acquisition, and churches/entities as External Power Bargains.

## Character start and death

- Every character starts Uninitiated and Factionless and needs Faction Entry; the Starting Faction is retired. All seven attributes start at 1, and the player spends 9 free points once or picks an 11-point Character Archetype ([07 Character Life](07%20Character%20Life.md)). Before the sealed being manifests, death uses a fixed Death Gap with world resolution and may produce a successor if at least one uncaptured city has no battle in it; otherwise death is final (Last Free City Rule). Once the being has manifested, player death is terminal defeat with no successor, even if the being is later defeated, though the world may continue. Either campaign completion route ends the campaign with a rating; whether play continues afterward with the surviving current character remains open. Successors stay unavailable after manifestation. V1 intentional inheritance is a simple Legacy Cache marker. Soft Memory may affect dialogue/opinion; no bloodline respawn.

## Items, inventory and recovery

- Item Creation and Enchanting are equipment/item-crafting professions; Alchemy is a separate potion-only profession that every character must learn before Arcana advancement. Brewing takes six world ticks and includes a mini-game; rules are in [20 Alchemy and Potioncraft](20%20Alchemy%20and%20Potioncraft.md). Luck and Perception are attributes, not professions. Item Creation stage gates and modifier-count formula are in [19 Items, Crafting, Inventory, Equipment, and Enchanting](19%20Items%2C%20Crafting%2C%20Inventory%2C%20Equipment%2C%20and%20Enchanting.md). Base-favored modifier-type weights stay fixed; item stage shifts tier weights.

- Enchanting's six-step order is random modifier addition, full reforge, locking, extraction/insertion, tier raising, and changing values of all unlocked modifiers while preserving their tiers. Insertion into any base requires a free slot in the extracted modifier's group. Broken items allow extraction only; no separate delete or replace operation.

- Personal inventory is an unlimited weight-only bag. The starting comfortable capacity is 20 kg + Strength, with a 200% hard carry limit; gold weighs zero. Durability applies to hand equipment, worn armour, and resource-gathering tools, but not jewellery or belts; repair uses ordinary creation ingredients and no rare ingredients.

- Ordinary monsters drop ingredients only. NPC equipment recovery is an independent per-item roll at a 35% base chance modified by the current looter's Luck, not Perception. A new Character Life does not inherit carried/equipped items; if the former character is encountered as a world actor and defeated, use the same recovery rule. Legacy Cache contents remain separate/open.

## Confront and Combat Grid

- V1 Confront is Turn-Based Mode **in place** on a **Combat Grid** over the real scene (BG3 / Fallout 1–2 feel, not feature parity), decided by the owner on 2026-11-01. The Combat Arena and Theme Packs are retired. No voluntary Turn-Based Mode during free exploration. See [16 Architecture Pivot](16%20Architecture%20Pivot.md) and [08 Confront Constraints](08%20Confront%20Constraints.md).

- V1 tactical combat takes place on the square Combat Grid during Confront only. Combat uses Initiative, an AP pool with variable action costs, and retained end-of-turn AP for defensive reactions as defined in [10.2 Combat System Draft](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md). The wider world advances by one shared tick after each complete combat round, not during an individual turn ([18 World Time and Living Simulation](18%20World%20Time%20and%20Living%20Simulation.md)). Overworld and interiors remain free third-person spaces.

- The square grid is spatial truth **during Confront only** ([10.1](10.1%20Grid%20World%20and%20Environment%20Actions.md)). Overworld/interiors are free 3P outside Confront. In the slice the grid reaches 20 cells from the player in every direction and follows the player every step, and fights elsewhere are simulated; a fighter who crosses its edge changes between the two from the next round. Interiors stay separate scenes, and a fight follows through a door like a level change in Fallout. The technical test holds and the owner accepted it (`unity/Assets/Plans/COMBAT_GRID_TECH_TEST.md`). The grid has 1 m cells, is built during a real fight, and reads the place, never the characters in it; 50 cells is the plan for later. Fences, boundaries, lamps, props, and trees get colliders. Cover works only against ranged attacks. During Turn-Based Mode the rest of the world lives only between rounds, one tick per round.

## Stealth

- Stealth is [10.3 Stealth and Detection](10.3%20Stealth%20and%20Detection.md): a special action, 10-cell Vision Cones with an angle set by Vigilance, and a Notice Meter set by Stealth skill and Dexterity against Perception. Perception shows the meter and Intelligence the cones. Darkness adds to Stealth skill, and noise is not simulated. An NPC that loses sight of the character checks the last place and stays Alert. Outside a fight allies break stealth too; in a fight they do not. A stealth attack rolls to hit as usual, adds 50 percentage points of critical chance, and never stuns; in combat, critical chance above the 95 % cap adds to the critical damage multiplier one to one.

## Items in fights

- Owner's direction, 2026-11-02: attributes differ only slightly, with nothing like ×2 between peers, and soldiers differ by a random spread. Preparation through whole sets of items decides fights, and items strengthen a character significantly. No single item or consumable may change a battle's result that much, so the Alchemical Flask, proposed the same day as a thrown potion with area damage, is rejected. The Modifier Catalogue in [19](19%20Items%2C%20Crafting%2C%20Inventory%2C%20Equipment%2C%20and%20Enchanting.md) §2.4 and Outfit V0 in [10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §5b are authoring defaults checked by `tools/balance/item_sim.py`: the allies alone stop the slice's ritual in about 40 % of runs, one more club adds 12 points against 29 with no items, and one Rare weapon on that fighter adds about 2. The owner decided on 2026-11-02 that the allies' edge stays with their Limit 8 fighters, who buy from the capital's Master while the cult's buy at the city shop; a cult Limit 9 stays a little stronger than an allied one.

## Shops and Masters

- Owner's direction, 2026-11-02: every city has shops of equipment, potions, First Aid, ingredients, and resources, and their keepers are the Masters who teach those professions. The capital's Masters are stronger, and the industry and artifact research faction has a more advanced Master with a shop. Both sides buy in the same shops, so no armourers are needed and the origin of a fighter's items needs no explanation. Shelves improve with the age of the world, and a Legendary stays far rarer than anything else at the start. Ingredients and resources differ by what lies around each city, and territories will be balanced by stage and difficulty, but not in the slice. Joining a faction may bring a Faction Gift, an item or several to choose from, Magic or Rare and very rarely Epic, random by seed. In the slice the player reaches gear by gathering, crafting, tasks, buying, and theft, with Enchanting left out and a Legendary very unlikely but possible.
- Working readings, not owner decisions: stock is made by the formula of [19](19%20Items%2C%20Crafting%2C%20Inventory%2C%20Equipment%2C%20and%20Enchanting.md) §3 at a Shop Skill of 26 in a city, 50 for a capital Master, and 75 for the faction's Master, rising by 1 a game day. Open points are CC-48.

## Wait and Defensive Stance

- Owner-approved, 2026-11-02: as in Heroes III, a fighter can wait, moving its turn to the end of the round, where those who waited act lowest Initiative first. An NPC with higher Initiative that cannot reach an enemy now, but will once that enemy has walked closer, waits. A fighter can go into defence instead of attacking, which raises its chance to evade, block, or parry in proportion and must not become a best move, and it answers an attacker with a Counterattack. Critical hits are ×2.
- Working readings in [10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §5b: each AP held in a Defensive Stance adds 1.5 Evasion and 3 % to block or parry, and a Counterattack costs the weapon's attack plus 2 AP. Hold Back is the agent's addition, because Waiting alone let a side with three Initiative modifiers win 90 % of fights.

## Kinds of fighter

- Owner's direction, 2026-11-02: fighters who go through critical hits, plain attack, magic, and ranged attacks are about equal overall and play against each other: mages are good against plate, shooters against mages, and plate against shooters, because arrows do not pierce physical armour. Critical hits stand level with the others, and Initiative is no automatic win. The style is Victorian: staffs and wands are cancelled, with no standard fireball; the crossbow is removed; gunpowder rifles and pistols, with a shot and a Reload, join swords, daggers, and hand weapons; and the bow stays for now. Characters of high Intelligence attack with the same items and fill their weapon with magic through Will Power: it adds magical damage, works with no spell, comes more often with more Will, and goes through heavy armour better. Magical weapons exist. Crafting has no ceiling: its price is time and effort.
- Authoring defaults and working readings in [10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §5b, checked by `tools/balance/build_sim.py`: physical defence counts double against ranged physical damage; Infusion costs a flat 5 × S a blow and adds `weapon base × Stage Factor × Intelligence / 24`; chain halves it and plate stops it; a melee critical hit halves physical defence; the load bands take Evasion and AP in a fight, so that Strength pays for heavy armour. Five builds of one budget reach 46–54 % five against five, with the triangle at 71, 88, and 72. Alone a shooter wins 27 % and a mage 34 %.
- Owner-approved, 2026-11-03: shooters and mages stay weak alone, against 60–65 % for melee builds, as the price of their strength in a group.
- Owner-approved, 2026-11-03: any character who has entered an Arcana can infuse, with no training; the power comes with initiation, and an Uninitiated character has none.
- Owner-approved, 2026-11-03: Infusion is paid for each blow, never once for a whole turn, so that a weapon with many blows a turn gains no cheaper Infusion. Other open points are CC-49.

## Campaign odds

- Owner-approved on 2026-11-02, in [21 Cult Campaign and City Control](21%20Cult%20Campaign%20and%20City%20Control.md) §8. Simulations always run with no player, and the world lives on its own. Both sides pursue their goals with the same seriousness. Allied factions act on goals as enemy NPCs do: they choose a city and forces, gather, and go; they reinforce a city under attack; they really try to retake captured cities while their forces allow; and they try to hold the capital at the end. They must defend the capital, so only a pool of forces is free. They post Liberation Contracts to other factions, Factionless characters, and otherworldly people. They have Limit 2 Allied Generals who want the Cult Generals dead. Their goddess on the Divine Plane can intervene now and then, with abilities up to Limit 0, through a Divine Blessing or strengthening of one or several direct followers, perhaps a global strengthening, never as constant attention. These events are how the war reaches the player. With no player the allied side wins at low settings, the middle setting is 40/60 in favour of the cult, and higher settings are 20/80 and worse. Only the player's actions can turn this: gear, progression, the quality of those actions, the help given in defences and liberations, and how far the character grows, possibly to Limit 2. The slice aims at about 40/60 with no player and has no easier or harder variant for now. The simulation gives 42/57 at the middle setting, 19/78 at the High setting, and 40 % for the slice's allies alone. Which numbers reach this are authoring defaults and working readings, kept in CC-43 and CC-44.

## Dialogue

- Dialogue checks are [10.4 Dialogue and Social Checks](10.4%20Dialogue%20and%20Social%20Checks.md), modelled on Disco Elysium: `2d6 + Character Value + Modifiers` against `Request + Listener Value`, +2 for an enemy, a deliberate exception to the curve of 07.1. A Social Check exists only with an Argument the character gained in this life; a fact the player remembers from an earlier life does not count until the new character finds it again. The slice has Persuasion and Deception on Intelligence and Will and Intimidation on Strength and Will, resisted by the listener's same attributes; Intelligence shows the percentage. Persuasion brings an ordinary gain and never money or items; Deception brings more and costs more. A failed check cannot be repeated on the spot: friendly listeners may give a clue or a Proof Task, and against enemies it is a Final Check that ends in a fight. The slice's readings of the Deeds Record are Reputation and Danger. Numbers are the agent's authoring defaults, because the owner left balance calculations to the agent.

## Overworld terrain stack

- **Overworld terrain stack:** **TerrainGen is the terrain authority and Unity `TerrainData` is the representation.** `OverworldTerrainBake` (Editor) runs one production path: `OverworldTerrainGrid` creates the **2×2 × 1400 m** tiles (**`TerrainHeightBudgetMeters` = 700**, heightmap 513, alphamap 512, details 512) → `TerrainAuthorityField` evaluates `IslandElevation` (`H4A`) onto `OverworldTerrainField`, one world-spanning lattice → a pending hydrology plan lowers the lattice (`HydrologyBake.ApplyPendingCarve`, hydrology off by default) → `OverworldTerrainBuilder` writes `SetHeights`, `SetAlphamaps`, `SetTreeInstances`, `SetDetailLayer`. Nothing is read back from `TerrainData` into TerrainGen. **Seams are exact by construction:** tile-edge vertices are shared lattice points, alphamap texels sit at `i / (resolution − 1)`, and every rule is a function of world position. **Terrain material = URP TerrainLit** (`Assets/Settings/OverworldTerrain.mat`) reading the `TerrainLayer` assets — **MicroSplat is out of the pipeline** (Core only, no URP adapter; its shader ignores `TerrainLayer.diffuseRemap*`/`tileSize`/`smoothness`, which is what made “snow” render as raw sand). **Surface layers** = canonical **5**-asset stack in `TerrainSurfaceLayer` order (grass / sand / soil / cliff / rock), textures under `Assets/Art/Terrain/SurfaceLayers`. **V0 water = ocean only** — one WaterWorks `WorldWater` at Sea Level 24 m (`OverworldWater.mat`). Vegetation = `OverworldTerrainBuilder` trees and ground cover over `OverworldVegetationAssets` prototypes (Polytope prefabs); there is **no** second grass system. Architecture: `unity/Assets/Plans/OVERWORLD_TERRAIN_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Unity terrain traps

- **Unity terrain traps:** `OverworldTerrainData_*.asset` is gitignored, so a local rebake leaves the saved scene's town standing on the old ground; `Overworld_Generator` is in that state now (2–45 m above it), so measure buildings on a town generated on the present terrain; a `TerrainData` must become an asset **before** its alphamaps are written — made an asset afterwards, its splat textures are recreated and the painted weights silently revert to the base layer; `SetDetailResolution` and the `heightmapResolution` setter **discard** existing data; a terrain tree prototype needs an **LOD Group or Mesh Renderer on the prefab root**; terrain details are invisible past `detailObjectDistance` (default 80 m, maximum 250 m); heights store at 16 bits over half the normalised range (a 2.14 cm step on a 700 m tile, so a written height is within 1.07 cm of its source) and splat weights at 8 bits, so equality tests need those tolerances.

## Terrain guardrails

- **Terrain guardrails (hard):** **one** production terrain pipeline — no fallback, no alternate bake, no compatibility adapter, no optional external generator. TerrainGen's Stage 3C/3D/3E topology is frozen; the terrain bake consumes the field and never changes it. **Stage 4A island relief (`IslandRelief`) is the production terrain authority** (revision R1, connected 2026-09-18, `GeneratorVersion` 2): `TerrainAuthorityField` evaluates `IslandElevation` — **`H4A = H1 + U2 + R4A`**. `R4A` is non-negative and exactly zero at and seaward of the shore, so connecting it moved no coastline and no ocean sample. **Stage 3D/3E still declare basins and outlets on `StructuralElevation` (`H2`)** — that is the surface their topology was frozen against, and Stage 3F will carve `H = H4A − C3F`; never repoint them at `H4A`. `includeMassifs: false` stays Stage 1 only (no Stage 2A means no macro plates for 4A to stand on). Its EditMode suite is green; residuals (zero knolls, apron ring/zone, coast-aligned gradient, curvature ratio, massif-foot sweep pits) are recorded in `OVERWORLD_TECH_DEBT.md` item 19 as closed-with-residuals owned by Stage 3F / Stage 4B — not as expected red tests, and not as things to retune inside 4A. `TerrainLayer` assets carry Inspector tuning and are created only when missing; their appearance is never set from code. Roads and settlements are the one sanctioned post-bake writer: they append **their own** layers after the bake and must never reorder or repaint the canonical indices 0–4.

## Road passability contract

- **Road passability contract (approved by the project owner 2026-08-31):** **mountain terrain is traversable terrain, not a hard obstacle.** Roads may cross mountain terrain; pathfinding should prefer cheaper terrain such as **passes, contours and switchbacks** over a direct expensive climb, and it expresses that as **price, not refusal**. Mountain terrain must **not** be converted into `+Infinity` merely because it is Mountain — `MountainPenalty` and `MountainCost` stay **soft**; never turn either into a blocker, and do not read «never straight through a mountain» as «mountains are impassable». Hard blocking is limited to explicitly impossible terrain and constraints: **water / `+Infinity`**, **earthwork beyond allowed limits** (`MaxCutDepth` / `MaxFillHeight`), and **grade >= `MaxWalkableGrade`**. **`ExistingCorridorDiscount` affects road construction cost / corridor-reuse preference only and must never change terrain passability** — read passability from `LandscapeCostField.TerrainCost` / `TerrainCostAtTerrain` (undiscounted) and construction cost from `Evaluate` / `EvaluateAtTerrain` (discounted, what A* prices). Source: `unity/Assets/Plans/WORLD_GENERATION_PIPELINE.md` §3; term in [90 Glossary](90%20Glossary.md) § Road Passability; decision in [99 Open Questions](99%20Open%20Questions.md) § Resolved Decisions. **Approved but not Canonical.** The owner review required by `001 Documentation Standards` § Promotion Rules is granted, and the remaining requirements (no contradictions, dependencies, Glossary, Open Questions) are met. What is missing is only a host: no Canonical document covers world generation, and the document carrying the statement is Draft with unrelated content.
