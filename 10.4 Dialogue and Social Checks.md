# Purpose and authority

This document defines the checks a character makes in conversation. It gives Convince and Threaten of [06 Approaches](06%20Approaches.md) their rules. It does not design dialogue trees or their content.

The owner chose Disco Elysium as the model for conversation on 2026-11-02: its calculation, its kinds of check, and its actions in dialogue, adapted to this game's attributes. The owner left the balance calculations to the agent, so every number here is an authoring default until playtests.

# 1. Arguments

A Social Check is not available by default. A character cannot lie to, persuade, or threaten any NPC at will.

A Social Check needs an **Argument**: something the character has learned or holds that gives the attempt a reason. It can be a clue, a fact, an item, or something the character saw. The character has to know what to threaten with, what to lie about, and what to base a persuasion on.

Without the Argument, the option does not appear.

- **Persuasion** needs a reason the listener can accept.
- **Deception** needs enough detail to make the lie believable.
- **Intimidation** needs something to threaten with. A fearsome name alone is not an Argument.

An Argument opens a particular check on a particular matter. It is not a key to every conversation.

The character has to gain the Argument in this life. A fact the player remembers from an earlier life is not an Argument until the new character finds it again. What the player remembers still helps: the player knows where to look.

# 2. Social Checks

A **Social Check** is a dialogue option with a chance of success. The slice has three, and the listener resists with the same two attributes the character uses.

| Skill | In Russian | Attributes of both sides | Closest in Disco Elysium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Persuasion | Убеждение | Intelligence, Will | Rhetoric |
| Intimidation | Запугивание | Strength, Will | Authority |
| Deception | Обман | Intelligence, Will | Drama |

Each is a skill and grows through practice. The full game has more (§8).

Working reading: Persuasion and Deception serve Convince, and Intimidation serves Threaten.

## Calculation

Social Checks use two dice, not the curve of [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md). This is a deliberate exception for conversation.

`Character Value = floor(((A + B) / 2 + Skill) / 20)`

`Listener Value = floor((A + B) / 20)`

`Difficulty = Request + Listener Value + 2 if the listener is an enemy`

`Success when 2d6 + Character Value + Modifiers ≥ Difficulty`

Values are whole numbers, so a few points of advantage change nothing. Raising the attributes and the skill by 10 each makes one step. The listener's own skill does not count.

The two dice always fail on 1 + 1 and always succeed on 6 + 6, so the chance stays between 3 % and 97 %. Luck multiplies the chance under [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md), up to the same 97 %. Social Checks have no critical outcome in V1.

## Request size

The size of what the character asks for sets the base of the Difficulty. The designer of each dialogue sets it.

| Request | Value | Examples | Chance at equal values |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Small | 5 | Share a rumour, step aside in a public place | 83 % |
| Ordinary | 7 | Tell a harmless secret, grant a small exception | 58 % |
| Large | 9 | Risk a post, go against an order, stop a ritual | 28 % |
| Huge | 11 | Betray a faction | 8 % |

## Modifiers

| Source | Modifier |
|---|---:|
| Attitude of this NPC | −2 to +2 |
| Reputation or Danger (§4) | −2 to +2 |
| Each further Argument on the same matter | +1, at most +2 |
| An ability such as Внушение | +1 |

The sum stays between −4 and +4. A player can read these modifiers in the option, as in Disco Elysium, once Intelligence allows it (§3).

# 3. What the player sees

The chance always exists. At first the player sees the options without any chance, and cannot tell how likely a lie or a threat is to work. Intelligence reveals it, under the progressive disclosure of [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md).

| Intelligence | What it shows |
|---:|---|
| Below 10 | The option only |
| 10 | The percentage of each check |
| 20 | The list of modifiers behind it |

# 4. Reputation and Danger

The game keeps a **Deeds Record**, a statistic of what the character has done, as in Space Rangers. Two readings of it matter in the slice.

**Reputation** is how good a name the character has with a faction.

| Deed | Points |
|---|---:|
| A task done for the faction | +3 |
| An NPC helped | +1 |
| A city liberated | +10 |
| A theft noticed | −3 |
| A break-in noticed | −5 |

| Reputation points | Modifier |
|---:|---:|
| −10 or less | −2 |
| −9 to −3 | −1 |
| −2 to +4 | 0 |
| +5 to +14 | +1 |
| +15 or more | +2 |

**Danger** is how dangerous the character is. NPCs can look at the character and judge it, so it needs no rumour.

`Danger = kills + 5 per Limit reached`

| Danger | Modifier |
|---:|---:|
| 0–2 | −1 |
| 3–9 | 0 |
| 10–19 | +1 |
| 20 or more | +2 |

Which reading applies depends on the skill and the listener:

| Skill | Ally or neutral listener | Enemy listener |
|---|---|---|
| Persuasion | Reputation | Danger. A villain respects a dangerous person, not a kind one |
| Intimidation | Danger | Danger |
| Deception | Reputation | A positive Reputation counts against the character, because the enemy knows whose side it is on |

**Attitude** is how this one NPC feels about the character. **Rumours** carry a name beyond those who met the character ([09](09%20Information%20Sources.md)). In the slice's one city everyone counts as having heard. In the full game a change of 5 Reputation points or more reaches another city after one game day for each tile of road between them, carried by travellers (authoring default, 2026-11-03).

Player Knowledge stays separate from the chance: a clue opens an option, it is not a general bonus.

# 5. What each skill brings

| Skill | On success | Its price |
|---|---|---|
| Persuasion | An ordinary gain toward the character's goal: information, passage, help. Never money or items | The mildest failure |
| Deception | A larger gain, money and items included | A larger consequence: failure costs 5 Reputation, and the listener warns others |
| Intimidation | Quick compliance | Attitude falls by 1 step even on success |

Authoring defaults, 2026-11-03: the listed costs, and Intimidation's whole row, which follows [06 Approaches](06%20Approaches.md), so Intimidation costs Attitude even when it works. A lie that succeeded may be exposed later, at the same cost: at the next day boundary it comes out at 20 % on an Ordinary request and 40 % on a Large one, and never on a Small one. An exposed listener's Attitude falls by 2 steps. A lie to an enemy who is dead or gone can no longer come out.

# 6. Failure

A failed Social Check cannot be tried again on the spot.

## Friendly and neutral listeners

The listener usually leaves a way forward, though not in every case.

- It may give a clue that lets the character reach the goal without a check.
- It may set a **Proof Task**: "prove you deserve this first." That can be a task, or a Reputation to reach. After it, the check opens once more or the listener simply tells the character.

## Enemies

A check against an enemy is a **Final Check**. There is no second try and no Proof Task. An enemy who can fight starts a fight at once. Trying to talk a cultist into derailing a ritual, or to trick one, ends in battle if it fails.

## Allies who can fight

Allied NPCs who can fight, such as guards, may start a fight too if the character keeps ignoring what they tell it to do. Authoring default, 2026-11-03: a guard gives two warnings, the second with a drawn weapon, and each leaves the character one tick to comply by stopping or leaving. A third time is a fight. Running out of its Vision Cone counts as fleeing, and the guard follows.

## Arrest

Owner-approved, 2026-11-06: a Warden who stops a character for a crime opens an **Arrest** ([10.3 Stealth and Detection](10.3%20Stealth%20and%20Detection.md) §6.2). The world pauses for it, as for any dialogue.

| Option | When | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Surrender | Always | A Yield ([10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §9): the lock-up until 08:00 or the invasion, and the weapons kept until the fine is paid. Stolen goods the character carries go back to their owners |
| Bribe | Always | Exchange, with no check. A corrupt Warden takes it and lets the character go. An honest one refuses, and the fine rises by what was offered |
| Deception | With an Argument | The evidence is the Argument: nothing stolen on the character, and the Warden did not see the crime itself ("I mistook the door"). So hiding the loot before the Warden arrives pays |
| Persuasion | With an Argument | The Wardens' debt from POI A or B ([11 Vertical Slice](11%20Vertical%20Slice.md)), a Reputation of +5 or more, or giving back what was stolen |
| Intimidation | With an Argument | Something that compromises this Warden or the Wardens |
| Fight | Always | Confront on the Combat Grid. The Wardens are allies, so the fight cancels their debt and counts as another noticed crime |
| Flight | Always | Leaving the Warden's Vision Cone is fleeing, and it follows (*Allies who can fight*) |

The rule of §1 holds: a social option without its Argument does not appear. A Warden is not an enemy, so a failed check is not a Final Check; it leaves Surrender, Bribe, Fight, and Flight, and each check is tried once. For Император, Surrender counts against the Arcana as any Yield does.

Authoring defaults, 2026-11-06: the request is Ordinary (7) when the Warden only heard of the crime and Large (9) when it saw it; the seed makes one Warden in three corrupt, and Information Sources can tell which; a corrupt Warden takes 500 bronze, half the fine.

# 7. Balance

The owner's requirement: a character slightly stronger than its enemies must not be able to persuade or deceive them at will.

Chance by lead, where lead is Character Value + Modifiers − Listener Value:

| Lead | Friendly Ordinary | Enemy Ordinary | Enemy Large |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| −2 | 28 % | 8 % | 3 % |
| −1 | 42 % | 17 % | 3 % |
| 0 | 58 % | 28 % | 8 % |
| +1 | 72 % | 42 % | 17 % |
| +2 | 83 % | 58 % | 28 % |
| +3 | 92 % | 72 % | 42 % |
| +4 | 97 % | 83 % | 58 % |
| +5 | 97 % | 92 % | 72 % |
| +6 | 97 % | 97 % | 83 % |

- A lead of 1 gives 42 % on an ordinary request to an enemy. Three such checks in a row all pass 7 % of the time.
- Near certainty needs a lead of 6 on that request: attributes and skill each about 60 above the listener's attributes, or the same lead built partly from modifiers. That is a veteran facing a recruit.
- Every check needs an Argument, and each one on an enemy is final, so the risk cannot be rerolled.
- Skill counts in the Character Value and not in the Listener Value, so attributes alone do not keep up: attributes of 50 with skill 20 give 3 against a listener's 5.

**In the slice.** A new character has a value of 0, and a prepared one at Limit 8 has 1. A resident has 0 or 1. A Limit 9 follower has 0. Followers of Император have little Intelligence, so against Persuasion and Deception a Limit 8 follower has 0 and a Limit 7 has 1, and against Intimidation they have 1 and 2. A new character asking a resident of value 1 has 42 % on an ordinary request and 72 % on a small one.

Stopping a ritual by talking to a conductor is a Large Final Check. At value 1 against a Limit 9 conductor's 0 it has 17 % with no modifiers, 42 % at +2, and 72 % at +4. Intimidating a Limit 8 senior, at value 1 against 1, has 8 %, 28 %, and 58 %. So the path works only for a character who prepared. The traces that give those Arguments are in [11 Vertical Slice](11%20Vertical%20Slice.md).

## Passive Checks

In Disco Elysium a skill can speak up without a roll. Adapted here, a **Passive Check** succeeds when 6 + Character Value + Modifiers reaches its Difficulty, and it gives a hint or a further line.

## Actions in dialogue

Dialogue can hold more than words: showing an item or evidence, or a physical act such as grabbing someone, which may start a fight. V1 has showing an item. The rest is for later.

# 9. Where Social Checks are used

- **Stopping a ritual** through its Ritual Weak Points, while the cult has not fully taken the city ([21](21%20Cult%20Campaign%20and%20City%20Control.md)).
- **A follower's demand** in a captured city ([21](21%20Cult%20Campaign%20and%20City%20Control.md)).
- **In a fight,** when the player opens a dialogue ([10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md)). It costs 2 AP (authoring default, [10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §5b).
- **With abilities.** Внушение adds to the next Persuasion check, and Чуткое ухо shows that a listener is lying ([05.1](05.1%20Arcana%20and%20Limits.md)).

# 10. Design check

- **Curiosity:** a check exists only for a character who found something out, so exploring opens conversations.
- **World:** what a character did returns through its Reputation and Danger.
- **Cost:** Deception pays more and risks more, an enemy gives one try, and a good name works against a character among enemies.

**Risk.** The designer sets the Request and writes the Arguments of every check, so this system costs authored content. Later cities and rituals need templates of them.

# 11. Scope

## V1

Arguments, the three checks, two dice, request sizes, Proof Tasks and Final Checks, the Arrest, Reputation, Danger, Attitude, the percentage shown by Intelligence, and showing an item.

## V2

The checks of §8, Passive Checks, physical actions in dialogue, and a Reputation for each faction. Rumours that travel between cities have their default in §4.
