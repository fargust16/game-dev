# Purpose and authority

This document defines the checks a character makes in conversation. It gives Convince and Threaten of [06 Approaches](06%20Approaches.md) their rules. It does not design dialogue trees or their content.

The rules below were approved by the owner on 2026-11-02, except where marked as a working reading.

# 1. Social Checks

A **Social Check** is a dialogue option with a chance of success. The player picks the option, and the game rolls under the shared action check of [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md).

The slice has three skills. The listener resists with the same two attributes the character uses.

| Skill | In Russian | Character's attributes | Listener's attributes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Persuasion | Убеждение | Intelligence, Will | Intelligence, Will |
| Intimidation | Запугивание | Strength, Will | Strength, Will |
| Deception | Обман | Intelligence, Will | Intelligence, Will |

Each is a skill and grows through practice.

Working readings:

- The two attributes count equally, and the listener's values stand in for task difficulty:

  `L_main = ((Attribute A + Attribute B) / 2 + Skill) / 2`

  `D = (Listener's A + Listener's B) / 2`

- The listener's own skill does not count.
- Persuasion and Deception serve Convince, and Intimidation serves Threaten.

# 2. What the player sees

The chance always exists. What changes is whether the player can read it, under the progressive disclosure of [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md).

At first the player sees the options without any chance, and cannot tell how likely a lie or a threat is to work. With enough Intelligence the player sees the percentage of each check. The threshold is open (CC-40).

# 3. What changes the chance

Three things change the chance of a Social Check.

- **Attitude** is how this NPC feels about the character.
- **Reputation** is what people have heard about the character, such as being a killer or a decent person.
- **Rumours** carry Reputation to people who never met the character ([09](09%20Information%20Sources.md)).

They change the chance and do not replace the roll. Player Knowledge stays separate: a clue opens an option or a line of reasoning, and it is not a bonus to the chance.

The effect depends on the skill and on who is listening:

| Skill | Ally or neutral listener | Enemy listener |
|---|---|---|
| Persuasion | A good Reputation helps | Needs a different Reputation. A good one does not help with a villain |
| Intimidation | A Reputation as a killer helps | A Reputation as a killer helps |
| Deception | A good Reputation helps, because people believe the character | May work the other way |

Working reading: Intimidation works the same on both sides.

Deception must not work quite like Persuasion. How exactly it differs is open (CC-40).

# 4. Failure

A failed Social Check still has to change something, under the failure rules of [03 Decision Engine](03%20Decision%20Engine.md) and [06 Approaches](06%20Approaches.md). Whether a character can try the same check again is open (CC-40).

# 5. Where Social Checks are used

- **Stopping a ritual,** while the cult has not fully taken the city ([21](21%20Cult%20Campaign%20and%20City%20Control.md)).
- **A follower's demand** in a captured city ([21](21%20Cult%20Campaign%20and%20City%20Control.md)).
- **In a fight,** when the player opens a dialogue ([10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md)). Its AP cost is open.
- **With abilities.** Внушение adds to the next Persuasion check, and Чуткое ухо shows that a listener is lying ([05.1](05.1%20Arcana%20and%20Limits.md)).

# 6. Design check

- **Curiosity:** a low-Intelligence player guesses at people and learns them by trying.
- **World:** what a character did earlier returns through what people have heard about it.
- **Cost:** a Reputation that helps with one listener hurts with another, so no name opens every door.

**Risks.**

- Persuasion and Deception use the same attributes. Only their skills and the Reputation rule separate them, so that rule has to do real work.
- The curve of [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md) falls to 1 % at a difference of 8. A new Оборванец with skill 1 against a listener at 10 and 10 has a difference of about −7.75, so about 1 %. These checks need the task-equivalent scale that 07.1 leaves open (CC-40).

# 7. Scope

## V1

The three skills, the opposed attributes, the percentage shown by Intelligence, and a simple Attitude and Reputation.

## V2

The other candidate social skills of [18 RPG Systems Coverage Draft](18%20RPG%20Systems%20Coverage%20Draft.md), richer rumours, and Reputation that differs by district or faction.
