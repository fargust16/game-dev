# Purpose and authority

This document defines how a character hides and how others notice it. It gives the Veil Approach of [06 Approaches](06%20Approaches.md) its rules.

The rules below were approved by the owner on 2026-11-02, except where marked as a working reading. Numbers marked as defaults are authoring defaults. The rule of [07.2](07.2%20Derived%20Character%20Statistics.md) still holds: there is no universal Stealth percentage, and a result always depends on the observer.

# 1. Stealth Mode

**Stealth Mode** is a special action. The character crouches and stays in it while nobody has noticed it.

Once someone notices the character, that NPC says a line over its head, such as asking what the character is doing, and the character stands up and leaves Stealth Mode.

A crouching character moves a little slower than a walking one: at three quarters of walking speed, and in a fight at 3 AP for two cells (owner-approved, 2026-11-03).

# 2. Vision Cone

Every NPC has a **Vision Cone**, pointing the way the NPC is facing or moving.

- **Range:** 10 cells, the same distance as leaving a fight ([18](18%20World%20Time%20and%20Living%20Simulation.md)).
- **Angle:** set by the **World Inhabitants** Difficulty Setting ([02 World Laws](02%20World%20Laws.md)), into which the owner folded the former Vigilance slider on 2026-11-03. A low setting gives a small angle. A high setting reaches a real human angle or slightly more. The default range is 60° to 130°.

A character outside every cone cannot be noticed by sight.

# 3. Notice Meter

Being inside a cone does not end Stealth Mode at once. It fills the observer's **Notice Meter**, a mark over the NPC with no numbers. When the meter is full, the NPC has noticed the character.

The meter fills slower the higher the character's Stealth skill and Dexterity are, and the lower the observer's Perception is.

**Darkness** is an extra modifier to Stealth skill. The darker the place, the higher the skill counts, so the character is harder to make out.

Noise is not simulated and plays no part in the meter.

Every NPC has this meter, whether or not the player can see it.

## Losing sight

An NPC that noticed the character and then lost sight of it goes to check the last place where it noticed the character. Its meter then fades slowly.

That NPC stays **Alert**. An Alert NPC gets an extra modifier to its detection, so its meter fills faster.

Working reading: a meter that never filled only fades, and the NPC goes nowhere.

## Numbers

Set by the agent and approved by the owner on 2026-11-03; playtest may still change them.

The meter compares the hider with the observer on the scale of [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md):

`Δ = (Dexterity + Stealth skill + Darkness) / 2 − Perception`, held between −4 and +4, with the observer's Perception × 1.25 when it is Alert.

`Time to fill = 3 s × 1.5^Δ × (0.5 + d / 10)`, where d is the distance in cells, so a meter fills nearly twice as fast in the next cell as at 5 cells, and one and a half times as slowly at the cone's edge.

- **Darkness** adds to Stealth skill: shadow or a dim lamp +2, a street at night with no lamp +4, an unlit room +6. Daylight and a lit lamp add nothing.
- **Alert** raises each NPC's own Perception by 25 %, by the owner's decision: a percentage, never a flat amount, so a sharp-eyed guard gains more than a dull one.
- **Fading.** Outside every cone a full meter empties in 8 seconds. An NPC that noticed the character and lost sight of it goes to the last place it saw it, and its meter empties in 16 seconds. It stays Alert for 10 ticks after its meter is empty; seeing the character again starts the time anew.

At 5 cells in the slice, against a Limit 9 guard with Perception 4:

| Hider | Place | Δ | Time to fill |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| New character, Dexterity 3, Stealth 1 | lit street | −2 | 1.3 s |
| The same | street at night, no lamp | 0 | 3 s |
| Жрица after two days, Dexterity 5, Stealth 5 | shadow | +2 | 6.8 s |
| The same, against an Alert guard, Perception 5 | shadow | +1 | 4.5 s |

A new character can slip past a guard only at night or behind its back, and practice makes a lit street possible. No hider is safe in front of a guard in the next cell, where even Δ +4 fills the meter in 9 seconds.

# 4. What the player sees

What the character can read depends on its attributes, under the progressive disclosure of [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md).

| Attribute | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Low Perception and Intelligence | Only the result: the NPC's line, and the character standing up |
| Higher Perception | The Notice Meter over the NPC |
| Higher Intelligence | The Vision Cones, faint at first and clearer as Intelligence grows |

Owner-approved, 2026-11-03: the Notice Meter shows from Perception 4. The cones show faintly from Intelligence 3 and clearly from Intelligence 6. So Сыщик, at Perception 6 and Intelligence 5, sees both from the start, and Боец sees neither.

# 5. Who breaks stealth

- **Outside a fight,** everyone counts, allies included. If an ally notices the character, stealth has failed.
- **In a fight,** allies do not break it.

# 6. Crimes in a peaceful city

Three things count as suspicious in a peaceful city: theft, breaking in, and entering a forbidden place.

- **Forbidden places** are those a guard would certainly know about, such as state buildings. Guards at gates and patrols know that only people on a list may enter, and they react to anyone else.
- **Theft from a person** may be noticed. Owner-approved, 2026-11-03: Theft from a person is one Reaction Check. Theft skill and Dexterity widen its good zone and the target's Perception narrows it; any result in the good zone takes the item unseen, and a bad one means the target notices. Owner-approved, 2026-11-03: theft has a good zone only, of width `20 % × 1.25^Δ`, with `Δ = (Dexterity + Theft skill) / 2 − Perception`, with Perception × 1.25 when Alert. Since 2026-11-11 the zone is held between 5 % and 35 % and the marker's pace follows Δ too, as every Reaction Check's does ([07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md) §8a); it was 1.5 seconds and at most 60 %. Owner's direction, 2026-11-11: a character can steal only in Stealth Mode. The world paused while the bar showed until the owner's second direction of the same day: since then theft is played like any other Reaction Check with the world running, and it is called off, neither noticed nor taken, when the person moves more than 1 m from where it stood when the theft began. A new character with Dexterity 3 gets 20 % against a townsman with Perception 2 and 13 % against a guard with Perception 4; a practised one with Dexterity 5 and Theft 5 gets 35 %, the widest, against the townsman. People react differently: one catches the thief, another runs off, a third goes to the guards. Reputation with local factions can fall.
  - *Working reading of AN3 (2026-11-12), the agent's:* the character crouches to reach the pocket and the bar shows where the hand reaches in. A try called off, by the person's metre or by a move key, is not spent, and the person may be tried again that day.
  - Owner's play test, 2026-11-12: a theft also fails when the person goes in at a door, and the screen says so ("Провал — цель зашла внутрь"). *Working reading, the agent's:* like a try called off by the person's metre, it takes nothing and is not noticed, so the try is not spent.
- **Breaking in.** Owner-approved, 2026-11-06: anyone who sees a character picking a lock from outside notices, because a lock picked in the street is plainly a crime. Inside a place, only those who live there and the guards posted to it notice; nobody else cares what the character is doing there. §6.1 sets out breaking into a dwelling.

A theft or break-in that someone notices counts against Reputation ([10.4 Dialogue and Social Checks](10.4%20Dialogue%20and%20Social%20Checks.md)).

**In the game (S5a.8).** Working readings, not owner decisions (`VERTICAL_SLICE_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`, S5a.8; Stealth Mode only since the owner's play test of S5a, with no pause since the second direction): the good zone lies whole on the bar but never in its first fifth, placed by the seed; each person can be tried once a game day; the walking townsfolk can be robbed now, and the named people, the Wardens and the merchants later; a noticed person cries out and notices the character as a Vision Cone does, and the three reactions above wait for the slice's townsfolk and the Arrest.

## 6.1 Breaking into a dwelling

Owner-approved, 2026-11-06: every dwelling of a city can be entered, and its door can be picked. Its rooms come from the Dwelling scene of [15 Procedural Building Generation](15%20Procedural%20Building%20Generation.md).

- **The lock** is one Reaction Check, as theft is: a good zone only, of width `20 % × 1.25^Δ` with `Δ = (Dexterity + Theft skill) / 2 − Lock`, held and paced by [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md) §8a. Unlike theft, it does not pause the world. Picking a lock is not a skill of its own in the slice; Lockpicking stays a candidate in [18 RPG Systems Coverage Draft](18%20RPG%20Systems%20Coverage%20Draft.md). Each attempt takes one tick. A good result opens the door. A bad one breaks a pick, and the character may try again with the next.
- **Lockpicks** are spent by failed attempts. Veinmarket sells a set of five for 200 bronze.
- **The Lock** follows the dwelling's wealth: 2 in a poor home, 4 in a middling one, 6 in a rich one. A new character with Dexterity 3 and Theft 1 gets 20 %, 13 %, and 8 %; a practised one with Dexterity 5 and Theft 5 gets 35 %, 25 %, and 16 %.
- **Residents** keep their schedules ([18 World Time and Living Simulation](18%20World%20Time%20and%20Living%20Simulation.md)): many are at work by day and asleep at home by night. A sleeping resident notices with half its Perception. Working reading: the evening, with residents at home and awake, is the worst hour.
- **Servants** (owner, 2026-11-07): a rich house may have servants at home. A servant is a resident like any other, with its own schedule, cone and meter, and a servant who witnesses a break-in runs out of the house to the nearest Warden (§6.2). One is at home 06:00–22:00 in every rich house and a second in half of them, asleep in the servant's room at night (owner, 2026-11-07; authoring defaults; [INTERIOR_FURNISHING_RULES.md](unity/Assets/Plans/INTERIOR_FURNISHING_RULES.md) §9). The numbers below count them (VI-6): each servant adds 3 percentage points to the chance of being noticed inside, an authoring default of `burglary_sim.py`.
- **What lies inside** reads from the people who live there, never from a lottery: the Stash of [19](19%20Items%2C%20Crafting%2C%20Inventory%2C%20Equipment%2C%20and%20Enchanting.md) §6.3.
- A picked door stays open until morning, and a Stash taken stays empty for the run.

## 6.2 Witnesses and the Wardens

Owner-approved, 2026-11-06:

1. A witness to a break-in runs to the nearest Warden on duty, or calls out if one is near. The character's Reputation falls at once, by the noticed break-in of [10.4](10.4%20Dialogue%20and%20Social%20Checks.md) §4.
2. The Warden goes to where the character was last seen. If it sees the character, it stops it and opens an **Arrest** ([10.4](10.4%20Dialogue%20and%20Social%20Checks.md) §6).
3. The character can hide and get away. Until 08:00 the next morning, any Warden who sees it stops it the same way (authoring default).
4. A victim of a noticed theft from a person who goes to the guards (§6) brings a Warden the same way.

**Numbers** (authoring defaults, 2026-11-06, checked again with the servants on 2026-11-07, by `tools/balance/burglary_sim.py`, whose witness and resident chances stand in for the cones and meters the game evaluates in place). A careful practised burglar who takes three rich dwellings in a night brings away about 2 000 bronze on average and spends one night in four in a cell; a new character brings about 1 150 on average, also one night in four, but more than half of its nights bring nothing. Robbing the poor does not pay, because the fine outweighs what poor homes hold. A greedy burglar who goes on all night is jailed about two nights in five on the Ridge and one in two elsewhere. The servants cost a rich night about a seventh of its takings and add about four points to the chance of a cell. Against about 5 000 bronze for a day's honest work ([19](19%20Items%2C%20Crafting%2C%20Inventory%2C%20Equipment%2C%20and%20Enchanting.md) §6.3), a night of burglary is half a day's pay, bought with sleep and a one-in-five chance of a cell.

# 7. Stealth attack

An attack from Stealth Mode strikes first. It rolls to hit as usual, so even a stealth attack can miss.

On a hit, it adds 50 percentage points to the calculated critical chance of [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md). The value may rise after playtests. The actual chance stays capped at 95 %, and what rises above the cap goes into critical damage, one to one ([10.2 Combat System Draft](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md)).

With Luck 0:

| Perception | Critical chance | Added to the critical damage multiplier |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 55 % | 0 |
| 25 | 77 % | 0 |
| 50 | 95 % | +0.05 |
| 100 | 95 % | +0.50 |

Working reading: the bonus is added after Luck, so Luck does not multiply it.

Initiative is rolled after that attack, whether it hit or not. A character who wins it acts again.

A stealth attack never stuns. Stun would make striking from hiding the answer to every fight.

A dagger adds 1.0 to the critical multiplier of a stealth attack (authoring default, [10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §5b).

# 8. Stealth in Turn-Based Mode

Two ways of hiding exist during a fight.

- **Завеса** gives stealth regardless of who is looking, until the character attacks or someone uses an ability that reveals it. The slice has no such ability. The full game will.
- **Stealth Mode** can be entered in a fight if no cone covers the character. Entering it costs 3 AP (authoring default, [10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) §5b). When a cone later touches the character's position, it is a check of the character's Dexterity and Stealth skill against the observer's Perception: the curve of [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md) at the Δ of § 3, without distance, so Δ −2 is 50 % and Δ +2 is 95 %. The character stays hidden or leaves Stealth Mode at once.

The cones need NPCs to have a facing on the Combat Grid, which [10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) has not defined yet.

**In the game (S3b.1).** Working readings, not owner decisions. The numbers above are on `StealthRules` and the stealth attack's on `FightRules`; the key, the eye heights and the observers' Perception are authoring defaults.

- **The key.** C enters and leaves Stealth Mode, in free movement and in a fight. The walk clip plays at three quarters of walking speed until a crouching clip exists ([ANIMATION_PIPELINE_PLAN.md](unity/Assets/Plans/ANIMATION_PIPELINE_PLAN.md)).
- **Who watches.** Every townsperson and named person watches with Perception 2, and the Wardens with Perception 4, as §6 and §3 give them, until S5b's sheets give NPCs their own. Street dogs do not watch.
- **Sight.** A cone sees from the eyes at 1.6 m to a crouching head at 0.9 m or a standing one at 1.6 m. Structure closes it, and people do not.
- **A standing character** fills no meter. It hides nothing and does nothing suspicious, until crimes (§6) fill the meter. An observer that noticed the character keeps its meter full while it sees it standing, and Stealth Mode cannot be entered under its eyes.
- **Losing sight.** The observer goes to the last place where it saw the character if the way there is clear. Otherwise it turns to look from where it stands. When its meter is empty it goes back to its round. The meters run on game time and stop while the world is paused, and Alert counts world ticks.
- **Darkness.** At night, while the lamps are lit, a street farther than 6 m from a lit lamp is dark, +4, and the edge of a lamp's light, beyond 3 m, is dim, +2. By day a place the sun does not reach is in shadow, +2. Interiors count as lit until their rooms can be dark, which the dwellings of S3b.2 and later need.
- **In a fight.**
  - Fighters face the nearest enemy from the start, and then the way they last moved or struck.
  - A cone checks once when it comes to touch a hidden fighter, and not again while it stays on it. The fight's check counts the Darkness of the cell.
  - Enemies neither choose a hidden fighter as a target nor strike it as it walks past.
  - An attack from Stealth Mode is a stealth attack and reveals the attacker. §7 names the attack that opens a fight, and the agent reads the same rule for any attack from hiding.
  - From Stealth Mode the character enters a fight hidden. It opens with a stealth attack only when an opponent stands in reach as the fight starts.
  - The character stays in Stealth Mode after a fight it ended hidden. NPCs do not hide in the slice.

# 9. Stealth skill

Stealth is a skill and grows through practice. Stealth skill and Dexterity help the character, and Perception helps the observer.

# 10. Design check

- **Curiosity:** a character can watch where others look and find a way past them.
- **World:** guards know their own places, and a city's rules show in who reacts to what. What a home holds tells who lives in it, and when they leave it decides when to go in.
- **Cost:** stealth can fail before the player knows it, and failing costs standing or starts a fight.

**Risk.** A character with low Perception and Intelligence hides blind and learns of failure only when it stands up. This is intended, but it makes Боец a poor hider and gives Сыщик its identity.

# 11. Scope

## V1

Stealth Mode, Vision Cones, Notice Meters, darkness, Alert NPCs, the three crimes, breaking into dwellings with witnesses and the Wardens' pursuit, the stealth attack, and stealth in Turn-Based Mode.

## V2

Abilities that reveal hidden characters, and richer reactions from witnesses: neighbours who remember, denser patrols after a theft, shops that know stolen goods, a fence.

## V3

Safes, dogs, and escape from a cell.
