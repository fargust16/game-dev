# Purpose and authority

This document defines how a character hides and how others notice it. It gives the Veil Approach of [06 Approaches](06%20Approaches.md) its rules.

The rules below were approved by the owner on 2026-11-02, except where marked as a working reading. Numbers marked as defaults are authoring defaults. The rule of [07.2](07.2%20Derived%20Character%20Statistics.md) still holds: there is no universal Stealth percentage, and a result always depends on the observer.

# 1. Stealth Mode

**Stealth Mode** is a special action. The character crouches and stays in it while nobody has noticed it.

Once someone notices the character, that NPC says a line over its head, such as asking what the character is doing, and the character stands up and leaves Stealth Mode.

# 2. Vision Cone

Every NPC has a **Vision Cone**, pointing the way the NPC is facing or moving.

- **Range:** 10 cells, the same distance as leaving a fight ([18](18%20World%20Time%20and%20Living%20Simulation.md)).
- **Angle:** set by the ninth Difficulty Setting, **Vigilance** ([02 World Laws](02%20World%20Laws.md)). A low setting gives a small angle. A high setting reaches a real human angle or slightly more. The default range is 60° to 130°.

A character outside every cone cannot be noticed by sight.

# 3. Notice Meter

Being inside a cone does not end Stealth Mode at once. It fills the observer's **Notice Meter**, a mark over the NPC with no numbers. When the meter is full, the NPC has noticed the character.

The meter fills slower the higher the character's Stealth skill and Dexterity are, and the lower the observer's Perception is.

Every NPC has this meter, whether or not the player can see it.

# 4. What the player sees

What the character can read depends on its attributes, under the progressive disclosure of [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md).

| Attribute | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Low Perception and Intelligence | Only the result: the NPC's line, and the character standing up |
| Higher Perception | The Notice Meter over the NPC |
| Higher Intelligence | The Vision Cones, faint at first and clearer as Intelligence grows |

The thresholds are open (CC-39).

# 5. Who breaks stealth

- **Outside a fight,** everyone counts, allies included. If an ally notices the character, stealth has failed.
- **In a fight,** allies do not break it.

# 6. Crimes in a peaceful city

Three things count as suspicious in a peaceful city: theft, breaking in, and entering a forbidden place.

- **Forbidden places** are those a guard would certainly know about, such as state buildings. Guards at gates and patrols know that only people on a list may enter, and they react to anyone else.
- **Theft from a person** may be noticed. People react differently: one catches the thief, another runs off, a third goes to the guards. Standing with local factions can fall.
- **Breaking in** is noticed only by the owner of the place or the guards posted to it. Nobody else cares what the character is doing there.

# 7. Stealth attack

An attack from Stealth Mode strikes first and adds 50 percentage points to the conditional critical chance of [07.1](07.1%20Character%20Attributes%2C%20Skills%2C%20and%20Action%20Checks.md), up to its usual cap of 95 %. The value may rise after playtests.

Initiative is rolled after that attack. A character who wins it acts again.

A stealth attack never stuns. Stun would make striking from hiding the answer to every fight.

# 8. Stealth in Turn-Based Mode

Two ways of hiding exist during a fight.

- **Завеса** gives stealth regardless of who is looking, until the character attacks or someone uses an ability that reveals it. The slice has no such ability. The full game will.
- **Stealth Mode** can be entered in a fight if no cone covers the character. When a cone later touches the character's position, it is a check of the character's Dexterity and Stealth skill against the observer's Perception. The character stays hidden or leaves Stealth Mode at once.

The cones need NPCs to have a facing on the Combat Grid, which [10.2](10.2%20Combat%20System%20Draft.md) has not defined yet.

# 9. Stealth skill

Stealth is a skill and grows through practice. Stealth skill and Dexterity help the character, and Perception helps the observer.

# 10. Design check

- **Curiosity:** a character can watch where others look and find a way past them.
- **World:** guards know their own places, and a city's rules show in who reacts to what.
- **Cost:** stealth can fail before the player knows it, and failing costs standing or starts a fight.

**Risk.** A character with low Perception and Intelligence hides blind and learns of failure only when it stands up. This is intended, but it makes Боец a poor hider and gives Сыщик its identity.

# 11. Scope

## V1

Stealth Mode, Vision Cones, Notice Meters, the three crimes, the stealth attack, and stealth in Turn-Based Mode.

## V2

Abilities that reveal hidden characters, and richer reactions from witnesses.
